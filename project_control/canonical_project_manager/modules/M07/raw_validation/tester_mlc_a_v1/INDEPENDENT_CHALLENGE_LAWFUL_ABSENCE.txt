Lane 3AT independent challenge: lawful absence / zero-applicability
Unit: M07-PROOF-MLC-A-V1

Challenge
---------
Consider a valid, promoted K12 bundle and authoritative state/context in which no
modifier is applicable. Independently determine which outcome numbered authority
requires:

1. emit the promoted baseline unchanged with a ModifierDecision and an attesting
   ModifierApplicationReceipt that records zero applied modifiers;
2. emit a distinct lawful-not-applicable or withheld outcome;
3. reject the application because a bundle member or applicable modifier is
   mandatory; or
4. treat absence of the entire bundle differently from an empty bundle or from a
   non-empty bundle whose state predicates all evaluate false.

Authority examined
------------------
- docs/03.1_DOMAIN_MODEL_WORKING.md:35-37 requires applicable typed contracts to
  declare mandatory/optional/not-applicable facts and every carried identifier's
  lawful-absence rule. It does not define the M07 outcome.
- docs/07.1_RUNTIME_OWNERSHIP_AND_CONSUMER_LEDGER_WORKING.md:179-184 requires M07
  deterministic semantic receipts and minimum carriage including active modifier
  identities and resolved coefficient snapshots. It does not state whether an
  empty active set is lawful or what output/receipt follows.
- docs/07.2_STATE_RISK_AND_REPLAY_WORKING.md:159-175 requires replay evidence to
  preserve explicit missing, unavailable, blocked, conflict or non-applicable
  states. It records such states after an owner has governed them; it does not
  choose M07's missing/non-applicable semantics.
- docs/10.1_VOCABULARY_AND_TERM_GOVERNANCE_WORKING.md:155-159 constrains when a
  modifier may act but does not define the no-applicable-modifier result.
- docs/11_NATIVE_ARCHITECTURE_BLUEPRINT_CORE.md:271-286 names lawful inputs,
  bounded outputs and proof classes but not the zero-applicability algorithm.

Result
------
No current numbered proposition distinguishes missing bundle, empty bundle, and
zero-applicability or defines the corresponding decision/receipt. The general
lawful-absence and replay rules require that the M07 owner define this meaning;
they do not supply it.

    41	## 9.3 Lineage field semantics
    42
    43	The DMP lineage block declares how the packet occurrence relates to authoritative evidence, packet occurrences, domain outputs, decisions, and replay context. It is not an untyped bag of identifiers.
    44
    45	The current lineage fields have these bounded meanings:
    46
    47	- `parentids`: identities of immediate causal upstream DMP packet occurrences from which this packet occurrence was produced;
    48	- `dependencyids`: identities of other explicitly consumed packets, state snapshots, decisions, receipts, configuration records, or governed dependencies that are material but are not represented as immediate causal parents;

    55	- `decisiontraceid`: identity of the governed decision trace when the packet carries or depends on a material decision.

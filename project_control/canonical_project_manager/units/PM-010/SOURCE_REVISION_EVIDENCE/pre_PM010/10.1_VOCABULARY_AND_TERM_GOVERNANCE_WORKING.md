## 10.1 Semantic authority and artefact hierarchy

Vocabulary governance begins with semantic understanding. It is not a string-matching, schema-validation, generator-parity, candidate-count, test-colour, or file-stamp problem.

The vocabulary authority chain is:

1. `docs/10_VOCABULARY_AND_TERM_GOVERNANCE_CORE.md` and this admitted working overlay own vocabulary law;
2. semantically accepted entries and amendments in `docs/vocabulary/vocabulary_registry.md` and this overlay record human-readable governed meaning;
3. `docs/vocabulary/CONSOLIDATION_WORKFLOW.md` governs the feeder and reconciliation process subject to this normative pair;
4. `src/nvda_desk/schemas/vocabulary.py` validates machine structure and exact collision rules;
5. `src/nvda_desk/vocabulary_builder.py` projects already admitted entries into the generated machine registry;
6. `data/vocabulary/canonical_vocabulary.json` is the committed generated enforcement projection;
7. candidate audits, review helpers, stamps and tests provide bounded evidence only.

A downstream schema, builder, generated JSON file, helper, receipt, candidate report, stamp, or passing test cannot create semantic admission or redefine a term.

`docs/reference/vocabulary_dictionary_system_audit.md` is historical evidence describing an earlier `main` commit and superseded paths/types. It is not current normative or implementation truth.

## 10.2 Durable-term scope

A durable term is a word or phrase intentionally used as a stable canonical project concept across one or more governed boundaries. This includes, where applicable:

- architecture surfaces and authority classes;
- domain objects, decisions, states and receipts;
- packet roles, protocol fields and typed carrier concepts;
- status, classification and lifecycle values;
- policy, playbook, risk, persistence, review and replay concepts;
- public or cross-module contract names and machine-enforced canonical slugs;
- recurring control concepts whose meaning affects acceptance, implementation status or proof.

Ordinary explanatory prose, grammatical connective text, quoted source wording, one-off examples, local variable names, private helper names, filenames, test specimen labels and incidental phrase fragments are not durable terms merely because a scanner can extract them.

A technical identifier can map to a durable term, but character similarity does not establish shared meaning. Conversely, two different labels can be synonyms only after semantic judgement establishes that they refer to the same governed concept in the same scope.

## 10.3 Mandatory semantic admission record

Before a candidate becomes a durable term, the reviewing LLM must read the relevant normative and implementation context in full and understand the concept as a human reviewer would.

The semantic admission decision must establish:

- the concept and why it is needed;
- its exact bounded meaning and scope;
- the semantic owner;
- the surfaces and contracts to which it maps;
- its relationship to existing terms, including distinctions that must remain visible;
- canonical label and slug;
- lawful aliases and disallowed or misleading phrases;
- category and lifecycle/status where applicable;
- whether it is structural, implementation, historical, compatibility-only, provisional, reserved, or active language;
- the evidence and rationale for admission, enrichment, aliasing or quarantine;
- the affected normative, generated, test and code surfaces.

No script, classifier, similarity score, embedding, regular expression, search result, schema validator, generator, test or language-model batch may replace this proposition-level semantic judgement.

Automation may inventory, normalise, compare, detect exact collisions, surface possible near matches, generate a proposed diff, validate structure, regenerate downstream artefacts and run proof. It may not certify meaning, novelty, synonymy, scope, ownership, completeness or admission.

## 10.4 Candidate action law

Every reviewed candidate must receive exactly one semantic action:

- `enrich_existing`: the concept is already governed and the accepted entry requires bounded clarification or metadata enrichment;
- `add_new`: the concept is semantically distinct, necessary and lawfully owned, so a new canonical entry is required;
- `alias_only`: the candidate is another lawful label for an existing governed concept and must not create a second canonical meaning;
- `quarantine`: the candidate is ambiguous, colliding, misleading, premature, compatibility-only, incidental, unsupported, or otherwise not lawful for canonical admission.

Those actions are semantic decisions, not consequences of token matching.

A mechanical helper may emit a `proposed_action` or review route. The current helper names such output `classification`; that name does not make it authoritative. In particular:

- no exact collision does not prove `add_new`;
- an exact token match does not prove `enrich_existing`;
- an alias-token match does not prove the concepts are semantically identical;
- a similarity or disallowed-phrase match does not decide the final disposition;
- the current helper status `admissible` means only structurally reviewable under its algorithm and must not be interpreted as semantically admitted.

Pure helper methods that construct a proposed `VocabularyDocument` do not admit terms, even when they are named `with_candidate_admission`. Only an accepted semantic decision followed by controlled normative and generated-artefact update can do that.

## 10.5 One semantic registry and one generated projection

The intended stable state is:

- one semantically reviewed human-readable registry of durable meanings;
- one generator-owned machine projection of those accepted entries;
- exact traceability between each generated entry and its semantic admission record;
- no durable term present only in code, tests, generated JSON, historical notes or an unreviewed candidate payload.

The human-readable registry and generated JSON serve different purposes and must not be treated as interchangeable:

- the human-readable registry explains meaning, scope, ownership, relationships and status;
- the generated JSON supports machine validation, exact collision checks and downstream enforcement.

Both must agree on the admitted concept set. A parity test between the builder and JSON proves only that code and generated output agree; it does not prove that either agrees with the semantically accepted registry.

Current alpha truth is not yet reconciled. The PM-001 current-truth census independently reproduced the current bytes using Unicode-preserving case-fold, ordered ASCII token runs `[a-z0-9]+`, and single-space joining:

- `docs/vocabulary/vocabulary_registry.md` contains 46 human headings;
- `data/vocabulary/canonical_vocabulary.json` contains 87 generated entries;
- 26 normalised labels match exactly across the human and generated sides;
- 20 normalised human-registry labels have no corresponding generated label;
- 61 generated labels have no corresponding human-registry heading;
- several explicit admissions recorded in sections 10.7 and 10.8 are absent from one or both projection surfaces;
- the hard-coded builder is the current practical source of the generated JSON and contains generated-only labels without human-registry traceability. This is projection-direction debt; it does not move semantic authority from the accepted human-readable doctrine to the builder or generated JSON.

These counts are inventory evidence only. Each mismatch requires semantic adjudication; it must not be bulk-merged or automatically suppressed. The normative baseline may record the mismatch truthfully, but vocabulary freeze cannot claim full semantic/machine parity until the mismatches are resolved.

## 10.6 Candidate audit, clean state and proof isolation

The deterministic synonym audit is a harvesting and escalation aid only. It can identify exact or heuristic phrase relationships that merit review. It cannot prove that the repo contains no semantic duplication, missing term, scope conflict, misleading alias or vocabulary drift.

A report with zero heuristic candidates means only that the configured scanner found no candidate under its current paths, token pattern, normalisation and score rules. It is not a semantic clean certificate.

Every candidate produced by the audit must be read in its source context before disposition. A candidate report must preserve path, phrase, nearby context or a resolvable location, nearest governed term, algorithmic reason and semantic decision record.

Candidate checking and semantic adjudication must be read-only with respect to governed source and canonical artefacts. A check may write only to an explicitly generated evidence destination that does not masquerade as pre-existing governed truth. Tests must establish and clean up their own generated output rather than mutating a tracked candidate artefact.

The vocabulary touch stamp may advance only after:

1. all semantic candidate decisions are recorded;
2. the accepted human registry is coherent;
3. the generator and machine projection reflect the accepted decisions;
4. blocking proof passes in the declared environment;
5. the check leaves governed tracked truth unchanged.

Current alpha evidence is not clean: the committed candidate report and stamp retain an older shared fingerprint and report zero candidates, while a read-only current `build_report()` returns a different fingerprint with `vocabulary_touched=true`. The exact configured-algorithm result must be reproduced from current bytes and recorded as candidate occurrences, not described as semantic completeness.

The audit's `resolution_required=llm_escalation`, mechanical `classification.action=quarantine`, and review status `quarantined` are feeder/proof outputs only. They preserve candidate visibility and block automatic admission; they are not final semantic dispositions. Each occurrence still requires contextual semantic adjudication under sections 10.3 and 10.4. The report must not be hand-edited to hide candidates, and a zero-candidate result would certify only the configured scanner state, not semantic cleanliness.

- `active bounded pack surface` [`add_new`] = the specific file that declares or constrains the active bounded pack for the current route

## 10.8 Current programme semantic admissions

The following recurring control concepts are admitted for the current normative-alignment programme:

- `semantic acceptance` [`add_new`] = proposition-level human/LLM judgement reached after full contextual reading, cross-document comparison, explicit reasoning and full reread; mechanical evidence may support but cannot decide it
- `implementation truth` [`add_new`] = directly evidenced present behaviour, composition, persistence, proof or status, kept distinct from target architecture and planned intent
- `normative baseline` [`add_new`] = the versioned accepted set of normative cores, working addenda, authority order, hashes, effective status and explicit unresolved reservations
- `alpha implementation` [`add_new`] = incomplete pre-operational implementation whose present behaviour must be stated honestly and which carries no production, paper-trading, durability or end-to-end maturity implication
- `packet occurrence` [`add_new`] = one emitted DMP carriage instance identified within its packet source context, distinct from upstream source and domain identity
- `modular monolith` [`add_new`] = one maintained deployable codebase partitioned into bounded modules with explicit ownership and contracts so implementations can change independently; it does not imply microservices, shared internals or unrestricted in-process coupling
- `composition root` [`add_new`] = the bounded assembly surface that constructs concrete dependencies and connects adapters, services, repositories and entry points without owning the domain decisions performed by those components
- `market_session_reference` [`enrich_existing`] = checked, versioned session/date/venue truth in the financial-calendar architecture
- `financial_event_reference` [`enrich_existing`] = checked, versioned scheduled financial-event truth in the financial-calendar architecture
- `operational_calendar_state` [`enrich_existing`] = append-preserved observed or authorised correction truth for named sessions/events, distinct from immutable reference
- `derived_runtime_calendar_context` [`enrich_existing`] = deterministic non-owning projection of selected calendar facts for a stated effective/as-of window

These admissions govern meaning immediately through this normative overlay. Their machine-registry projection and proof belong to the later vocabulary implementation/proof work and must not be pre-empted by manual generated-JSON edits.

## 10.9 Freeze criteria

The vocabulary portion of the normative baseline can freeze when:

- the durable-term scope is consistently understood across the normative corpus;
- candidate routing is separated from semantic admission;
- every retained and current admission has a complete semantic record;
- contradictory or stale authority claims are explicitly superseded;
- the human registry has one accepted meaning per durable concept;
- all unresolved semantic collisions or omissions are visible and assigned to a later gate;
- no automated clean report is represented as semantic certification.

Full human-registry-to-generated-registry parity is a downstream implementation/proof obligation. Its absence must remain explicit in the freeze manifest and block any claim that vocabulary implementation is complete.

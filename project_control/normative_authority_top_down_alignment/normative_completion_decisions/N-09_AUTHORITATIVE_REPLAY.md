# N-09 — Authoritative replay envelope

## Unresolved proposition

## Adopted project decision

Authoritative replay is a governed reconstruction or isolated semantic re-execution of a named decision/transition from verified retrievable authoritative artefacts, complete result-changing versions and ordered state/transaction evidence. The minimum envelope preserves the consumed source/capture material, data/window and temporal truth, contracts/schemas, preparation/resolution/stage material, policies/configuration/coefficient/playbook/algorithm/executable versions, ordered packets/decisions/state/transactions/receipts, explicit missing/degraded/conflict state and the original bounded result/fingerprint.

Exact preserved occurrence replay, governed semantic re-execution and comparison-only activity are distinct. Newly emitted replay output receives new occurrence identity and references the original. Re-execution uses isolated state and controlled time, performs no live side effects and excludes emission metadata from semantic equivalence. Missing or unverifiable mandatory material yields `non_authoritative_incomplete`; comparison remains available but cannot be promoted into reconstruction authority.

Governing propositions: `docs/07.2_STATE_RISK_AND_REPLAY_WORKING.md` sections `07.2.N09.1` through `07.2.N09.7`.

## Alternatives ruled out

- Comparing two arbitrary mappings as end-to-end replay: ruled out because the original governed inputs/state/versions are not reconstructed.
- Treating a packet ID, hash, local object or fixture path as sufficient evidence: ruled out because referenced content must be retrievable and fingerprint-verified.
- Re-executing current code/config against current data: ruled out because exact result-changing meaning and retained evidence are mandatory.
- Calling a newly emitted replay packet the original: ruled out by packet occurrence identity law.
- Repeating external effects during replay: ruled out; retained request/acknowledgement/reconciliation evidence is consumed instead.
- Normalising a mismatch away or repairing it with current data: ruled out because mismatch is material evidence.

## Longitudinal effects

Retained authoritative artefacts and ordered state/version evidence enter an isolated replay run; verified executable meaning reproduces or withholds the bounded result; an authority assessment records exact matches, mismatches, missing material and no-side-effect status; comparison remains a downstream analysis tool.

## Transverse effects

The decision fixes replay class, envelope identity, source/data/time evidence, contract/config/policy/executable versions, state/transaction order, original/new occurrence identity, semantic equivalence, incomplete status, side-effect prohibition, extension law and alpha truth.

## External sources

No external source was required. The replay semantics are project-specific and were resolved from the governed ownership, packet, state, persistence, proof and alpha-truth laws.

## Current implementation consequence

Current replay services are comparison/analysis seams with optional lineage and process-memory sidecars. They do not implement authoritative reconstruction. No implementation or persistence change and no conformance claim is made.

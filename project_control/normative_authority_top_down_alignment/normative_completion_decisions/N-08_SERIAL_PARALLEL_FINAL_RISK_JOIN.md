## Adopted project decision

The final-risk join requires two complete independent branches: serial candidate/execution-expression permission/risk material and the exact Parallel Risk Lane packet with identity, policy/version, fingerprint and hard/caution/evidence state. It emits only `eligible_for_capital_assessment` or `stand_down` and grants no capital or execution authority.

Missing/invalid/contradictory branch authority, any serial hard block/non-permission, or any Parallel Risk hard block produces `stand_down`. Cautions are retained and may constrain later assessment but cannot authorise, erase a hard block or substitute for a missing branch. Numeric confidence never overrides categorical severity. The immutable receipt retains both branch lineages, every reason/constraint, join policy and output fingerprint.

Governing propositions: `docs/07.2_STATE_RISK_AND_REPLAY_WORKING.md` sections `07.2.N08.1` through `07.2.N08.6`.

## Alternatives ruled out

- Checking only for a lead playbook: ruled out because no Parallel Risk product is joined.
- Serial result overriding Parallel Risk due to confidence or completeness: ruled out because the lane is independent and hard blocks dominate.
- Parallel Risk caution creating positive permission: ruled out because caution is constraint/evidence, not authority.
- One branch substituting for the other: ruled out because the join exists specifically to require both independent owners.
- Collapsing all reasons into a 0/1 confidence scalar: ruled out because owner, severity and constraint meaning disappear.

## Longitudinal effects

Completed serial candidate/expression and independent Parallel Risk product enter the join; deterministic conjunctive precedence emits an immutable eligibility/stand-down receipt; capital authority later consumes the receipt and all constraints but remains free to decline deployment.

## Transverse effects

The decision fixes branch identity, independence, hard/caution severity, missing/conflict handling, output vocabulary, no-sizing boundary, extension law, reason provenance and deterministic replay.

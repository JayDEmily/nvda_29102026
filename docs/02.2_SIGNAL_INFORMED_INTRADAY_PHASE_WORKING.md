## 02.2.N07 Signal-informed intraday phase model

### 02.2.N07.1 Behavioural purpose and output states

The intraday phase interpreter represents the desk's evolving judgement about where the market is within the current trading session. It must distinguish structural clock/session position from observed market behaviour and must not declare that opening price discovery has ended merely because a fixed wall-clock threshold passed.

The admitted detailed phase states are ordered:

1. `pre_open`;
2. `opening_price_discovery`;
3. `opening_transition`;
4. `continuous_session`;
5. `closing_transition`;
6. `closing_auction`; and
7. `post_close`.

`unresolved` is an explicit non-phase outcome when the required authority is missing, contradictory or insufficient. It must not be treated as one of the ordered market phases.

The output must also carry the clock/session prior, evidence quality, confidence or bounded certainty state, transition reason, policy version, state/history version and the source/capture identities and observation interval used.

### 02.2.N07.2 Required input families

A signal-informed phase decision requires source/session temporal truth and venue/session-state reference plus admitted recent observations from each of these signal families:

- **activity and participation:** trade/quote activity or volume participation relative to an admitted session baseline;
- **liquidity and spread:** quoted spread and at least one admitted liquidity/depth or executable-liquidity proxy;
- **price discovery and instability:** short-horizon price movement, realised volatility or another admitted price-discovery/intensity measure; and
- **market-structure status:** auction/session status, halt or venue-state evidence where the source family provides it.

The market-structure status family is mandatory when the venue/source contract provides authoritative status; its absence must otherwise be explicit. Event/calendar proximity, options state, information-flow state and macro context may enrich phase evidence but do not substitute for the three core behavioural families.

Every signal observation must retain signal-family identity, source/capture identity, schema and derivation version, effective/observed time, freshness/availability state, unit and validation state. Provider-shaped raw payloads and generic mappings must not enter the phase policy.

### 02.2.N07.3 Clock prior versus behavioural authority

The session calendar and source time establish:

- whether the venue is before, inside or after the admitted session;
- the lawful ordering of phase states;
- a prior expectation about which transitions are plausible; and
- a fallback coarse clock bucket when behavioural evidence is unavailable.

Clock position alone may determine `pre_open`, `closing_auction` or `post_close` only where authoritative venue/session status makes that boundary factual. Inside the continuous trading session, a fixed time may open or close a transition-eligibility window but may not by itself force `opening_price_discovery` to become `continuous_session` or force a behavioural transition between detailed phases.

A clock-only result must be labelled `clock_only_degraded`, expose the coarse clock prior separately, and set the detailed signal-informed phase to `unresolved`. It must not masquerade as a signal-informed classification.

### 02.2.N07.4 Stateful transition semantics

The interpreter is stateful within a session. It must consume:

- prior accepted detailed phase and state version;
- time spent in the current phase;
- a bounded recent history of the admitted signal-family evidence;
- current evidence quality and freshness; and
- the versioned transition policy.

A forward transition requires policy-defined sustained evidence that the next phase's behavioural conditions are present. Entry and exit conditions must use hysteresis, minimum evidence duration or an equivalent anti-chatter mechanism. Exact thresholds, baselines, lookback lengths and dwell periods are versioned policy/configuration, not fixed normative numbers.

The normal phase sequence is monotonic. A phase must not regress because of one noisy observation. An apparent regression may instead change confidence, evidence quality or risk context. A sequence correction is lawful only when authoritative venue/session or source correction proves that the earlier session state was wrong; the correction and superseded state must remain in lineage.

A transition may skip an intermediate state only when observations were unavailable for the skipped interval and the retained evidence proves the later state; the gap and skip reason must be explicit. Otherwise the interpreter must not jump phases for convenience.

### 02.2.N07.5 Evidence interpretation by transition

The policy must define behavioural evidence for each transition without reducing it to clock cut-offs:

- `pre_open` -> `opening_price_discovery`: authoritative session open plus activity/participation and price-discovery evidence consistent with active opening formation;
- `opening_price_discovery` -> `opening_transition`: sustained reduction or stabilisation in opening intensity across activity, liquidity/spread and price-discovery families, subject to hysteresis;
- `opening_transition` -> `continuous_session`: sustained continuous-market conditions in those families, not merely elapsed time;
- `continuous_session` -> `closing_transition`: closing-period eligibility plus sustained change in participation, liquidity/spread or auction-related behaviour under the policy;
- `closing_transition` -> `closing_auction`: authoritative auction/session status when available, otherwise the explicitly admitted substitute evidence defined by the source contract and policy;
- any in-session phase -> `post_close`: authoritative session close/status or an admitted calendar boundary with no contradictory venue evidence.

The policy may use weighted or rule-based evidence, but it must expose the contributing signal-family states and reasons. A single proxy must not silently stand in for all required families.

### 02.2.N07.6 Conflict, missing data and degradation

Stale, missing, quarantined or invalid observations reduce evidence quality and cannot be silently imputed from wall clock, capture time, a previous session or another signal family.

If one core behavioural family is temporarily unavailable, the policy may hold the prior accepted phase with reduced confidence for a bounded, versioned grace interval. It may not assert a forward behavioural transition during that interval unless the policy expressly proves that the remaining evidence is sufficient for that exact transition.

If evidence families materially conflict, freshness is inadequate, prior state is unavailable when required, or the grace interval expires, the detailed phase becomes `unresolved` or remains at the last accepted phase with an explicit `held_degraded` status according to the versioned policy. Consumers must be able to distinguish held state from newly confirmed state.

Missing mandatory source/session truth, unknown policy version, invalid state sequence or contradictory authoritative venue status fails closed and withholds a valid phase context.

### 02.2.N07.7 Ownership, consumers and replay

The temporal domain owns the phase-state meanings and transition policy. Source adapters and preparation own typed signal admission and derivation. A session-scoped phase-state repository owns accepted prior state/history. The temporal service coordinates evaluation but does not own upstream signal meaning or downstream trading decisions.

Serial posture, playbook/expression stages and Parallel Risk Lane may consume the phase output as context. They must not reinterpret `unresolved`, `clock_only_degraded` or `held_degraded` as positive permission. Their own policy determines the bounded consequence of degraded temporal context.

Authoritative replay must retain the input observations or immutable references, session/calendar version, prior state, transition-policy version, thresholds/baselines/configuration, transition evidence, output state and state sequence. Replay must reproduce the same phase transition without using current wall-clock or live market data.

### 02.2.N07.8 Current alpha consequence

Current temporal code converts source time into a supplied timezone and applies fixed 09:30, 10:30, 15:30 and 16:00 clock thresholds to emit coarse `session_state`. That output is a deterministic clock classifier only. It does not implement or prove the admitted signal-informed intraday phase model, transition history, evidence quality, hysteresis or policy-version lineage.

This is an implementation consequence only. No production source, runtime configuration, schema, fixture or migration change is authorised here.


### 02.2.N07.9 Canonical alpha contract and M05 integration amendment

The canonical alpha implementation home is
`nvda_desk.domain.temporal.intraday_phase`. It owns immutable
`IntradayPhaseEvidence`, `IntradayPhasePolicy`, `IntradayPhaseState` and
`IntradayPhaseResult` contracts, the deterministic `IntradayPhaseEvaluator`, and
an injected session-scoped repository protocol. The process-memory repository is
alpha-only and must return detached copies; it does not prove restart durability.

M03 remains the owner of admitted typed signal observations and source/prepared
lineage. The temporal domain owns phase meanings, transition policy and accepted
session state/history. M05 owns only the narrow temporal-stage adapter that
translates one validated typed phase result into admitted primitive carrier facts
and an exact temporal authority reference. `SerialRunner` remains pure and does
not read or write the phase repository.

`unresolved`, `clock_only_degraded` and `held_degraded` results are explicit
non-permission. The M05 adapter must expose their missing/degraded state rather
than manufacture a positive temporal fact. Identical evidence, prior state and
policy must reproduce the same semantic result without current wall clock or live
data.

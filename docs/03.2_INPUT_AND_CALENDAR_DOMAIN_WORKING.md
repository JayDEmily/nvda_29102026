## 03.2.N01 Typed options material and derived-only cognition boundary

### 03.2.N01.1 Boundary decision

Provider-shaped or otherwise raw options material terminates at the source-owned ingress adapter. It must not cross into serial cognition, Parallel Risk Lane, capital, downstream placement, execution, inventory, close, review or replay comparison as an ungoverned mapping or provider-specific object.

Between input admission and runtime preparation, options material must use an options-family typed contract. That contract preserves the received options evidence and its identity, temporal truth, version and validation state. It is distinct from the stage-specific `options and flow context` input used by serial cognition.

The serial `options and flow context` stage consumes only typed derived primitives required by that stage, explicit missing or unavailable facts, and a lineage reference to the prepared options material through the enclosing governed context. It does not consume the raw chain, raw surface, provider response, source mapping or a generic dictionary. The exact common-versus-stage-specific field allocation is governed by package `N-05`; that allocation may not remove the lineage reference required here.

### 03.2.N01.2 Required options-material facts

The typed options-material contract must declare and preserve, with exact mandatory/optional/not-applicable status for the admitted source family:

1. options input-family and source-contract identity;
2. source/provider identity and acquisition channel;
3. source-issued observation, surface, chain or artefact identity when the source supplies one;
4. repository-owned capture identity for the received material;
5. input schema version and preparation-policy version;
6. effective time, observed time and captured time under the temporal meanings below;
7. payload or captured-material fingerprint sufficient to identify the admitted evidence;
8. validation outcome, source limitations and explicit missing facts;
9. the typed payload variant used by preparation.

A source-issued identifier and a repository capture identifier are different facts. Neither may be invented from a packet ID, trace ID, run ID, timestamp, filename, hash or local sequence merely to satisfy the other role.

### 03.2.N01.3 Options temporal meanings

For options material:

- **effective time** is the market instant, interval or date for which the options values are asserted to apply;
- **observed time** is the source-stated time or date at which the source formed or published the observation;
- **captured time** is the repository/process time at which the exact material entered the governed system.

The source contract must state which temporal facts it supplies and whether effective and observed time are identical for that source. Source precision must be preserved. Date-only truth must not be upgraded to an instant, and captured time must not substitute for missing effective or observed time.

### 03.2.N01.4 Stage-specific derived contract

The serial options-stage input may contain only:

- options-derived primitives lawfully required by the stage;
- typed categorical or quantitative states derived by preparation;
- explicit missing, unavailable or not-applicable facts;
- the governed context/lineage reference required to locate the prepared options material and its identity/version/time facts.

It must reject or withhold:

- raw/provider options fields that are not part of the admitted stage contract;
- nested generic mappings or arbitrary extension keys;
- candidate, risk, capital, placement, execution, inventory, close or review authority;
- fabricated source, capture, schema, policy or temporal facts.

A derived primitive must not be treated as self-authenticating. Its owning prepared material and derivation policy must remain traceable even though the raw payload does not cross the cognition boundary.

### 03.2.N01.5 Failure and degradation

If mandatory options-material identity, version or temporal truth is absent, invalid or contradictory, the system must fail closed at admission or preparation and must not present the serial options stage as having valid options context.

If the source contract lawfully permits a bounded missing fact, that absence must be explicit. The stage may emit an unavailable or incomplete options assessment only where its typed contract admits that state; it must not silently replace missing options truth with market-snapshot values, wall-clock time, a default provider, a local identifier or an empty mapping that appears authoritative.

### 03.2.N01.6 Ownership and substitution

The source adapter owns provider translation. Runtime preparation owns derivation and the options-material-to-stage transformation. The serial options stage owns only its bounded assessment from admitted derived inputs. Packet builders and downstream consumers carry the result and lineage; they do not recreate missing options evidence.

A provider or options input family may be replaced through its typed adapter and contract registration boundary without changing the serial stage contract unless the stage's governed information requirement itself changes through numbered normative authority.

### 03.2.N01.7 Supersession and relationship

This package resolves the ambiguity recorded for Gate C requirement `C-004` by adopting a derived-only serial cognition boundary while retaining typed options material and traceable identity/version/time before that boundary.

It extends, and does not replace:

- `docs/03.1_DOMAIN_MODEL_WORKING.md` raw-input identity, typed-carrier and source/prepared separation law;
- `docs/04.1_TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE_WORKING.md` adapter, source-time and typed-payload law;
- `docs/07.1_RUNTIME_OWNERSHIP_AND_CONSUMER_LEDGER_WORKING.md` owner, lineage and replay-envelope law;
- `docs/09_DMPV3_PROTOCOL_CORE.md` and `docs/09.1_DMPV3_PROTOCOL_WORKING.md` packet carriage law.

It does not define the common prepared envelope (`N-03`) or final common-versus-stage-specific field allocation (`N-05`). Those packages must preserve the boundary and lineage obligations established here.


## 03.2.N02 Financial-calendar layer model and ownership

### 03.2.N02.1 Exact four-layer model

The governed financial-calendar architecture has exactly four business layers:

1. `market_session_reference` — checked, versioned reference facts describing trading dates, venue/session states, holidays, half-days and admitted session boundaries;
2. `financial_event_reference` — checked, versioned reference facts describing scheduled financial, issuer, macroeconomic, expiry and other admitted event timing;
3. `operational_calendar_state` — append-preserved runtime observations or authorised corrections about a specific session or financial event after reference publication;
4. `derived_runtime_calendar_context` — a deterministic, context-only projection assembled for a stated effective/as-of window from selected facts in the first three layers.

These are semantic layers, not storage technologies or folder names. An in-memory repository, SQLite table, checked-in file, API response, packet and projection service are possible implementations or carriers; they do not create extra business layers.

The first two layers are distinct fact families. Neither has general precedence over the other. A financial event may affect runtime interpretation of a session, but it does not rewrite market-session truth. A session holiday or half-day may affect how an event is interpreted, but it does not erase the event fact.

### 03.2.N02.2 Layer owners and permitted authority

`market_session_reference` is owned by the checked calendar-reference authority for session facts. Its authorised writer is the admitted reference import/update process. Runtime consumers must treat a selected bundle/version as immutable.

`financial_event_reference` is owned by the checked calendar-reference authority for scheduled-event facts. Its authorised writer is the admitted reference import/update process. It is distinct from operational event observations even when both describe the same named event.

`operational_calendar_state` is owned by the operational calendar state boundary. Its authorised writer may append an observed fact or an explicit correction, cancellation, confirmation or actual-state record. It must not silently overwrite reference history or originate candidate, risk, capital, placement, execution, inventory, close or review authority.

`derived_runtime_calendar_context` is owned by the calendar projection boundary. It may select, relate, tag and project admitted facts for later temporal, serial and Parallel Risk consumption. It is not an authoritative writer for any source layer and must not write its derived result back as reference or operational truth.

### 03.2.N02.3 Identity and version requirements

Every reference layer must identify its layer name, bundle identity, bundle version, source artefact identities, source versions, contract/schema version and applicable effective range. Each fact must have an identity unique within its declared owner/namespace and must trace to its source artefact.

Every operational fact must identify its operational fact identity, fact family, observed/effective time, capture or writer identity, source/lineage identities, contract/schema version and whether it confirms, supplements, corrects, cancels or supersedes another named fact.

Every derived runtime context must identify its context identity, projection-policy version, effective/as-of window, selected source fact identities and versions, applied supersession decisions, conflict and completeness state, and deterministic projection fingerprint where claimed.

A layer ID, event ID, session ID, bundle ID, artefact ID, context ID and packet ID are different roles. One must not be fabricated from another to conceal missing authority.

### 03.2.N02.4 Precedence and substitution

Precedence is fact-family and semantic-key specific; there is no global source-order shortcut.

For a single session or event semantic key:

1. compatible reference facts from the same selected version may co-exist and must retain per-fact provenance;
2. a later reference version replaces an earlier version only when the selected bundle/version authority expressly chooses it; historical versions remain identifiable;
3. an operational fact may supersede one named reference or earlier operational fact only when it carries explicit supersession lineage, matching fact family and semantic key, an effective interval, an authorised writer/source, and a reason/status that the layer contract admits;
4. an operational observation that merely supplements a reference fact co-exists and does not overwrite it;
5. two incompatible facts at the same admitted precedence without a lawful supersession relationship are an unresolved conflict and no authoritative value may be selected for that semantic key;
6. `derived_runtime_calendar_context` applies the versioned projection policy to these preserved facts but never creates precedence by itself.

Freshness alone, file order, insertion order, source-list order, higher materiality, a later capture time or a non-empty value must not decide precedence unless the governing layer contract and projection policy explicitly authorise that fact-specific rule.

One layer must not substitute for another. In particular, absence of financial-event evidence must not be represented as a clear market-session layer; operational state must not masquerade as immutable reference; and a derived context must not be reused as source truth.

### 03.2.N02.5 Failure and graceful degradation

If the selected `market_session_reference` cannot resolve one authoritative session fact required for the target time, the system must withhold a valid runtime calendar context for that session and fail closed at the calendar boundary.

If the `financial_event_reference` source is unavailable or its completeness cannot be established, the context must state that financial-event completeness is unknown. It must not represent the condition as `no events`.

If `operational_calendar_state` is unavailable, the system may project from checked reference layers only where the projection contract permits that bounded mode. The result must record operational state as unavailable and must not assume that no correction exists.

An unresolved same-key conflict must be preserved as conflict evidence. If the conflicted fact is mandatory for the requested context, the context is withheld. If the projection contract permits a partial context, the affected fact is unavailable and the context is explicitly degraded; downstream decision owners determine their own fail-closed response without the calendar layer manufacturing a trade decision.

### 03.2.N02.6 Provider replacement and persistence separation

A calendar provider may be replaced only inside the typed adapter/contract for the same business layer. Replacement must preserve layer name, fact semantics, identity, version, time, validation and lineage obligations. A provider replacement cannot change the four-layer model or promote operational/derived material to reference truth.

Persistence implementations remain replaceable behind each layer's owning repository or reference contract. Process-memory and bounded SQLite implementations are alpha implementation truth only. They do not alter semantic ownership, prove durability, or create precedence between the four layers.

### 03.2.N02.7 Supersession and relationship

This package resolves the missing authority recorded for `C-005` and `C-018`. It replaces arbitrary business-layer naming with the exact four-layer model above while preserving lawful technical decomposition inside each layer.

Existing references such as `checked calendar/reference`, `operational event/session`, `PrecursorRuntimePacket`, `RuntimeCalendarSnapshot` and storage-specific session rows remain valid implementation evidence only when mapped to one of the four governed layers. They are not competing fifth or sixth business layers.

This package extends, and does not replace, `docs/03`, `docs/03.1`, `docs/04`, `docs/04.1`, `docs/07`, `docs/07.1`, `docs/11` and `docs/11.1`. It preserves calendar separation from candidate formation, risk ownership, capital, placement and execution. Package `N-04` governs canonical non-calendar merge policy and does not override the calendar-specific precedence law here.

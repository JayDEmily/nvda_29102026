## 03.2.N01 Typed options material and derived-only cognition boundary

### 03.2.N01.1 Boundary decision

Provider-shaped or otherwise raw options material terminates at the source-owned ingress adapter. It must not cross into serial cognition, Parallel Risk Lane, capital, downstream placement, execution, inventory, close, review or replay comparison as an ungoverned mapping or provider-specific object.

Between input admission and runtime preparation, options material must use an options-family typed contract. That contract preserves the received options evidence and its identity, temporal truth, version and validation state. It is distinct from the stage-specific `options and flow context` input used by serial cognition.

The serial `options and flow context` stage consumes only typed derived primitives required by that stage, explicit missing or unavailable facts, and a lineage reference to the prepared options material through the enclosing governed context. It does not consume the raw chain, raw surface, provider response, source mapping or a generic dictionary. The exact common-versus-stage-specific field allocation is governed by package `N-05`; that allocation may not remove the lineage reference required here.

### 03.2.N01.2 Required options-material facts

The typed options-material contract must declare and preserve, with exact mandatory/optional/not-applicable status for the admitted source family:

1. options input-family and source-contract identity;
2. source/provider identity and acquisition channel;
3. source-issued observation, surface, chain or artefact identity when the source supplies one;
4. repository-owned capture identity for the received material;
5. input schema version and preparation-policy version;
6. effective time, observed time and captured time under the temporal meanings below;
7. payload or captured-material fingerprint sufficient to identify the admitted evidence;
8. validation outcome, source limitations and explicit missing facts;
9. the typed payload variant used by preparation.

A source-issued identifier and a repository capture identifier are different facts. Neither may be invented from a packet ID, trace ID, run ID, timestamp, filename, hash or local sequence merely to satisfy the other role.

### 03.2.N01.3 Options temporal meanings

For options material:

- **effective time** is the market instant, interval or date for which the options values are asserted to apply;
- **observed time** is the source-stated time or date at which the source formed or published the observation;
- **captured time** is the repository/process time at which the exact material entered the governed system.

The source contract must state which temporal facts it supplies and whether effective and observed time are identical for that source. Source precision must be preserved. Date-only truth must not be upgraded to an instant, and captured time must not substitute for missing effective or observed time.

### 03.2.N01.4 Stage-specific derived contract

The serial options-stage input may contain only:

- options-derived primitives lawfully required by the stage;
- typed categorical or quantitative states derived by preparation;
- explicit missing, unavailable or not-applicable facts;
- the governed context/lineage reference required to locate the prepared options material and its identity/version/time facts.

It must reject or withhold:

- raw/provider options fields that are not part of the admitted stage contract;
- nested generic mappings or arbitrary extension keys;
- candidate, risk, capital, placement, execution, inventory, close or review authority;
- fabricated source, capture, schema, policy or temporal facts.

A derived primitive must not be treated as self-authenticating. Its owning prepared material and derivation policy must remain traceable even though the raw payload does not cross the cognition boundary.

### 03.2.N01.5 Failure and degradation

If mandatory options-material identity, version or temporal truth is absent, invalid or contradictory, the system must fail closed at admission or preparation and must not present the serial options stage as having valid options context.

If the source contract lawfully permits a bounded missing fact, that absence must be explicit. The stage may emit an unavailable or incomplete options assessment only where its typed contract admits that state; it must not silently replace missing options truth with market-snapshot values, wall-clock time, a default provider, a local identifier or an empty mapping that appears authoritative.

### 03.2.N01.6 Ownership and substitution

The source adapter owns provider translation. Runtime preparation owns derivation and the options-material-to-stage transformation. The serial options stage owns only its bounded assessment from admitted derived inputs. Packet builders and downstream consumers carry the result and lineage; they do not recreate missing options evidence.

A provider or options input family may be replaced through its typed adapter and contract registration boundary without changing the serial stage contract unless the stage's governed information requirement itself changes through numbered normative authority.

This package resolves the ambiguity recorded for Gate C requirement `C-004` by adopting a derived-only serial cognition boundary while retaining typed options material and traceable identity/version/time before that boundary.

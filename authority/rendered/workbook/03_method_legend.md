## Non-empty cells by row

**Row 1:** `A1`=80-Step Academic Evidence Audit | `B1`=Purpose and interpretation
**Row 2:** `A2`=Objective | `B2`=Test each of the 80 propositions against recognised peer-reviewed academic work or a recognised academic working-paper series. The workbook is an evidence audit, not a claim that the integrated trading system is profitable.
**Row 3:** `A3`=Direct | `B3`=The cited paper directly tests or derives the principal mechanism of the step. The exact NVDA implementation remains untested unless explicitly stated.
**Row 4:** `A4`=Partial | `B4`=The paper validates a material component, but the step combines additional mechanisms or operational requirements.
**Row 5:** `A5`=Adjacent | `B5`=The paper supplies a recognised theoretical or methodological foundation but does not directly validate the operational step.
**Row 6:** `A6`=Not found | `B6`=No suitable recognised academic example was located. No rows currently use this label; Adjacent rows are the weakest evidence found.
**Row 7:** `A7`=Paper reuse | `B7`=A strong paper may validate multiple propositions. Each row states the distinct proposition-level use rather than treating a citation as blanket approval.
**Row 8:** `A8`=Source standard | `B8`=Peer-reviewed journals were preferred. DOI links are included. The audit avoids blogs, forums and vendor marketing as evidence.
**Row 9:** `A9`=Important boundary | `B9`=Academic support for a component is not evidence of tradable alpha, correct coefficient values, correct data engineering, or successful live execution.
**Row 10:** `A10`=Known limitation | `B10`=The spreadsheet is a first-pass literature validation. It does not constitute a systematic review, meta-analysis, replication study or legal/financial advice.
**Row 11:** `A11`=Audit date | `B11`=2026-08-05
**Row 13:** `A13`=Addendum handling | `B13`=Steps 81–94 are kept separate from the original 80-step audit so the original sequence and evidence record remain unchanged.
**Row 14:** `A14`=Addendum gate | `B14`=PASS means the capability has defensible external support for inclusion. It does not validate the eventual equation, coefficient, threshold, signal weight or implementation.
**Row 15:** `A15`=Standards evidence | `B15`=Where a proposition is fundamentally a financial-data representation standard rather than an empirical market claim, an authoritative industry standard may be used as Direct evidence.
**Row 16:** `A16`=Addendum audit date | `B16`=2026-08-07

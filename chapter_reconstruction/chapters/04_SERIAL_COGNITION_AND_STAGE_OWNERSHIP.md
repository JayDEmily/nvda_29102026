# Chapter 4 — Serial Cognition and Stage Ownership

## Chapter role

This chapter defines the system's serial reasoning spine: the ordered questions it answers, the information each stage may own, the state it may retain, and the intermediate truths that must survive later mutation.

**Primary propositions:** `P-ARCH-001`, `P-ARCH-006`, `P-ARCH-008`, `P-SERIAL-001`, `P-CTX-003`, `P-ARCH-004`, `P-TRACE-006`, `P-DEC-002`.

## 1. Original intention: authority follows explicit cognitive ownership

The original workflow map rejects authority by convenience. A component does not own a decision simply because it happens to possess the data required to make it. The design must explicitly assign:

- Step-0 calendar and horizon routing;
- candidate-generation boundaries;
- execution-expression ownership;
- carry-branch boundaries;
- every cognitive stage answer and handoff.

Ambiguous or duplicated authority is invalid. It remains an unresolved boundary until assigned; it is not silently awarded to the nearest downstream stage. (`P-ARCH-001`)

This ownership law is the basis for later distinctions between posture and modifiers, eligibility and candidate selection, serial cognition and parallel risk, final risk and capital, and runtime output versus public projection.

## 2. Typed coordination develops without creating a new policy owner

The successor corpus strengthens the boundaries through typed contracts. Execution lifecycle, execution coordination, final-risk overlay, parallel-risk assessment and outbound projection cross their admitted seams through typed contracts rather than arbitrary dictionaries. (`P-ARCH-006`)

The runtime orchestrator validates those typed surfaces in governed order. It is a coordinator, not a super-stage. It may sequence, validate and route; it may not absorb the policy owned by temporal classification, regime, options/flow, posture, eligibility, expression, final risk or capital. Small services remain coordination owners. Public output shape derives from admitted contracts rather than whichever mapping keys happen to be present.

`StageLocalHandoffSurface` becomes the live execution-to-final-risk seam, but its existence does not move decision authority into the handoff object. A typed carrier can preserve truth; it does not own the truth it carries.

## 3. The serial spine is stateless

The serial cognitive spine evaluates the current admitted input bundle and emits its ordered answers without hidden memory. Temporal remediation, packetisation or orchestration cannot introduce implicit state, observation history or persistence feedback into a serial stage. (`P-ARCH-008`)

State and history belong to explicit downstream or sibling concerns. This does not prohibit a later explicit persistence or observation service. It prohibits a serial stage from changing its answer because of concealed internal memory that is absent from its contract.

The admitted corpus does not define a general cache policy. Any cache would therefore have to preserve the same explicit-input semantics and could not become undeclared decision state.

## 4. Current form: six ordered cognitive questions

The successor clarification fixes the serial stage order as: (`P-SERIAL-001`)

1. **Temporal** — What is the current signal-aware temporal and event-window state?
2. **Market regime** — What regime truth is supported by the prepared substrate?
3. **Options and flow** — What does the admitted options/flow evidence say?
4. **Posture and risk permission** — What hard constraints, permission and local capital envelope apply?
5. **Playbook eligibility** — Which families, variants and playbook IDs are admissible or watch-only?
6. **Expression and execution** — Which admitted candidates are adjudicated, which lead is selected and what execution expression is formed?

Each stage owns only its bounded answer and emits a lawful typed, inspectable output. The stages cannot collapse into one opaque bundle. Parallel risk, terminal risk, capital and downstream operations cannot absorb serial meaning merely to simplify orchestration.

The six-stage sequence is the serial cognitive spine. Earlier handoff authority also preserves an existing seven-stage compatibility/review order in the artefacts it amended. That preservation does not authorise a seventh serial cognitive owner or a change to the six bounded questions; the distinction among serial stages, terminal-risk/review sequence and packet-emitting stages remains explicit in later chapters.

## 5. Precursor context develops from presence into economics

A precursor packet is not informative merely because its fields are populated. It carries both aggregated raw-field presence and derived economic meaning. Financial-calendar precursor projection routes through market-state synthesis, and the result must distinguish supportive, contradictory and hostile states. (`P-CTX-003`)

Those distinctions matter because posture and review must be able to show different consequences. A binary “available/not available” marker would erase the economic interpretation and could falsely present contradictory evidence as support. Missing economic content is not supportive by default.

The admitted corpus does not state the complete precursor formulae or classification thresholds, but it does establish that availability alone is insufficient.

## 6. Posture ownership is partitioned before downstream mutation

Posture evaluation materialises three separate surfaces: (`P-DEC-002`)

- `hard_invariants` — posture-owned hard-block truth;
- `local_envelope` — base permission, base posture label, deployable-capital envelope and posture-owned derisk reasons;
- `downstream_annotations` — selector citations, modifier notes and other non-posture-owned annotations.

This partition prevents later reasoning from contaminating posture-owned truth. A modifier note cannot be inserted into `hard_invariants` merely because it contributed to the final outcome. A selector annotation cannot become a posture-owned derisk reason. Flat posture fields and a historical reasons list may remain only as compatibility representations of the partitioned authority.

The partition is evaluated before modifier application and before terminal risk. It does not change the final outcome or stage order; it makes the ownership that produced the outcome recoverable.

## 7. Intermediate stage truth must survive later mutation

The original runtime sequence was vulnerable to semantic overwrite: later mutation could leave review with only the final object and no trustworthy account of which stage owned which earlier answer. The repair therefore requires the runtime to preserve: (`P-ARCH-004`)

1. cited posture before modifier mutation;
2. cited eligibility before execution;
3. execution before modifier mutation;
4. execution after modifier mutation but before terminal risk;
5. the terminal-risk decision before final execution reshaping.

Exposing only the final mutated objects is insufficient. Review must be able to distinguish what posture decided, what eligibility admitted, what execution formed, what the modifier changed and what terminal risk then did.

## 8. `StageLocalHandoffSurface` carries the five preserved truths additively

The additive handoff surface carries the exact stage-local artefacts as: (`P-TRACE-006`)

- `cited_posture_pre_modifier`;
- `cited_eligibility`;
- `execution_pre_modifier`;
- `execution_post_modifier_pre_final_risk`;
- `final_risk_decision`.

The handoff is not a reconstructed narrative generated after the fact. It carries the actual preserved stage-local objects into runtime results and review. Missing or mutated seam values break inspectability; no fallback reconstruction from final prose is authorised.

The addition must not change final allow/derisk/block outcomes, modifier authority, pre-final-risk compatibility fields, terminal-risk application, serial order, review order or packet lineage order. The earlier source's preserved seven-stage order remains untouched within its bounded scope; the later six-stage clarification remains the current description of the serial cognitive spine.

## 9. Current authoritative position

The serial workflow now has these settled properties:

- each cognitive question and major boundary has an explicit owner;
- typed contracts carry coordination truth but do not own policy;
- the orchestrator coordinates and validates without becoming a super-stage;
- the serial spine is stateless;
- six ordered stages produce bounded inspectable answers;
- precursor context carries economic meaning, not field presence alone;
- posture separates hard invariants, local envelope and downstream annotations;
- five exact stage-local artefacts survive later mutation in an additive handoff;
- making the seams visible must not change decisions, order or packet lineage.

## 10. Unresolved matters

The corpus does not fully restate every stage schema, every precursor calculation, a general cache policy or final package/module allocation. Chapter 2 also preserves an unresolved question over which boundary ultimately owns regime-source completeness. None of those gaps permits the serial runtime to reach into preparation internals, fabricate missing stage inputs or absorb downstream policy.

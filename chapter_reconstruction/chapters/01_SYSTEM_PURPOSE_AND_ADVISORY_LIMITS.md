# Chapter 1 — System Purpose and Advisory Limits

## Chapter role

This chapter establishes the authority ceiling for the entire design. The system may collect, derive, classify, compare, rank, preview and explain. Those capabilities do not, by accumulation, become live trading, booked financial truth, autonomous optimisation or capital authority.

**Primary propositions:** `P-CTX-001`, `P-OPT-001`, `P-CTX-002`, `P-PLAY-001`, `P-EXE-001`, `P-DEC-001`, `P-REV-001`, `P-REV-002`, `P-RES-001`, `P-LEG-001`.

The exact source lineage remains in the linked proposition records and the canonical field register. This chapter is a narrative projection, not a replacement authority.

## 1. Original intention: a typed advisory desk

The earliest admitted design describes a deterministic and typed decision-support system for the NVDA desk. It is intended to combine market, options, temporal, posture, playbook and execution context into inspectable outputs. Its defining discipline is not the number of signals it can produce, but its ability to state:

1. what information it received;
2. what transformation it performed;
3. what the resulting output means;
4. what provenance supports it; and
5. what authority the output does **not** possess.

The first market-context surface therefore includes macro score, macro-adaptive context, peer divergence, volume-spike context, Asia-precursor context, engine score and signal-scan summaries. `run_signal_scan` is a typed wrapper around scan intent, a configured desk window and candidate-count summaries. It is not a scheduler, daemon, broker trigger or live-trading approval. Missing upstream provenance remains missing rather than being converted into an inferred score. (`P-CTX-001`)

Options context was added on the same basis. Quote-chain data, metadata and realised-volatility inputs may support typed gamma-pressure, IV-versus-realised-volatility, skew-inflection, volatility-corridor and VIX-spread surfaces. Proxying is lawful only where a deterministic source surface exists. Absent dependencies remain absent or fenced; the options layer has no broker, execution or approval authority. (`P-OPT-001`)

## 2. Development: richer context without authority inflation

The design then introduced higher-order composites: options-behaviour clustering, execution-context scoring, compression-regime detection and OBV/VI flow confirmation. Their purpose is descriptive synthesis. They do not create a new decision tier.

The boundary is explicit. OBV/VI confirmation must fail or fence when the required OBV tape is unavailable. A higher-order composite cannot silently widen into an archetype, ladder, named playbook, entry permission or execution trigger. The richer the derived description becomes, the more important it is to preserve the distinction between **information about a decision** and **authority to make or execute that decision**. (`P-CTX-002`)

That distinction continues at the first candidate-facing boundary. Archetype matching and tagging operate only on candidates that already exist. The entry gate may veto under current permission or event-window stress, but it cannot invent a candidate or playbook and cannot grant execution approval. No candidate means there is nothing to manufacture. (`P-PLAY-001`)

## 3. Execution-shaped outputs remain previews

The system was deliberately allowed to produce execution-shaped advisory material without claiming live execution. Ladder construction, fill-bias adjustment, VVIX shaping and volatility-sentiment context form a readiness layer. VVIX may reshape the current preview ladder, not a broker order. Volatility sentiment faces posture and interpretation, not placement authority. (`P-EXE-001`)

Confidence and conviction are bounded in the same way. Signal-conflict detection, confidence scoring and conviction-tier allocation are advisory classifications. They cannot be presented as hidden certainty, broker approval or capital-deployment permission. Uncertainty must remain visible rather than being laundered into a decisive score. (`P-DEC-001`)

This chapter therefore establishes a governing negative rule used throughout the later chapters:

> An output does not gain stronger authority merely because it resembles a downstream artefact.

A ladder is not an order. A candidate is not an authorised position. A confidence tier is not capital. A review result is not booked truth.

## 4. Review and feedback remain operator-facing

The review layer was introduced as an explanatory and diagnostic surface. P&L summaries, attribution, module scoring and daily summaries are retrospective descriptions over preview records. They do not establish settlement, booked P&L or decision authority. Missing evidence stays explicit rather than being backfilled with a financial conclusion. (`P-REV-001`)

Variant tracing, performance summaries, macro alignment and confidence-divergence logging likewise remain operator-facing diagnostics. They are not self-optimising loops. A tail-hedge injector may produce an advisory hedge candidate, but cannot claim that protection was placed, booked or automatically routed. (`P-REV-002`)

Later chapters make review and trace substantially richer, but this initial authority boundary survives: improved observability may explain a decision; it may not silently become a new decision-maker.

## 5. Historical material is evidence, not native runtime authority

The successor corpus confronted imported legacy material directly. The lawful default is quarantine. Historical imported artefacts may remain available as evidence, but native runtime must not import them as current authority and native tests must not treat them as native design law.

A bounded native bridge is possible only when a real live dependency proves that outright retirement would be false. Even then, the bridge must be the minimum advisory, contract or registry surface needed to preserve the dependency. It may not recreate the historical subsystem wholesale or take ownership of the serial runtime. No specific bridge is deemed necessary merely because legacy material exists. (`P-LEG-001`)

This is a general pattern: continuity is permitted, but continuity must be labelled and bounded. Historical existence does not equal current authority.

## 6. Scenario and observability package homes remain unresolved

The successor briefs define a two-branch law for proposed scenario and observability native families:

- **Bounded admission** is lawful only if a prior admissibility audit proves that a genuine bounded native owner already exists and that admitting the package would describe current behaviour rather than fabricate capability.
- **Reserved/containment** is the lawful result when that proof is absent: the package remains absent and the absence is made explicit through bounded proof or reporting, not through an empty placeholder package.

The external current-code, workbook and report evidence needed to select the branch was not admitted into the reconstruction. The result therefore remains unresolved. It is not lawful to promote the default into unconditional retirement, and it is equally unlawful to create a package merely to make the architecture look complete. (`P-RES-001`)

The constraints on any future bounded admission are already settled. A scenario family could not become live trading, broad orchestration or unbounded replay/API expansion. An observability family could not own business decisions, packet mutation, persistence policy or runtime orchestration. Scenario identifiers, trace evidence and ordinary logging do not by themselves prove that a native package owner exists.

## 7. Current authoritative position

The current design boundary is:

- context and options surfaces are typed, deterministic and provenanced;
- unsupported inputs and dependencies remain absent or fenced;
- derived composites remain descriptive;
- eligibility may admit or veto but cannot invent candidates or grant execution authority;
- readiness, confidence and conviction remain advisory;
- review and feedback remain descriptive and non-actuating;
- legacy imports remain quarantined unless a minimal bridge is proven necessary;
- scenario and observability package-home decisions remain conditional and unresolved;
- broker credentials, network placement, acknowledgements, booked positions, settlement, booked P&L and autonomous mutation are outside the admitted design unless a later proposition expressly grants them.

The remainder of the chapter set develops the system inside that ceiling. None of the later technical detail should be read as an implied escape from it.

## 8. Unresolved matters

The admitted corpus does not state the exact formulae and thresholds for several context scores, archetype matches, entry-gate vetoes, readiness geometry, confidence tiers, hedge-candidate generation or review scoring. It also does not determine which branch applies to the scenario and observability package homes, nor whether any particular legacy bridge is required.

Those gaps are explicit design Unknowns. They are not permissions to invent defaults.

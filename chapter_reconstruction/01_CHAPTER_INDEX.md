## Dependency sequence

1. Chapters 1–3 establish the system boundary, information truth and temporal truth.
2. Chapters 4–6 develop serial reasoning, decision formation and packet carriage.
3. Chapters 7–8 apply independent risk, capital and lifecycle/persistence constraints.
4. Chapter 9 makes the resulting authority and behaviour inspectable.
5. Chapter 10 closes with the cross-cutting governance, compatibility and public-boundary law.

This order is not a claim that governance occurs last at runtime. Chapter 10 is placed last because it governs all preceding chapters and is most intelligible after the governed surfaces have been introduced.

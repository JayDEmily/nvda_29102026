## Continuous execution — no routine reapproval

A single accepted and commissioned plan covers its in-scope eligible work through verified close-out. Execute the next authorised leaf, review, checkpoint or handover without requesting a separate 'continue' instruction. Do not confuse an evidence/review gate with a new owner permission gate.

At a real unresolved invariant, stop dependent actions, preserve the exact blocker and continue independent authorised work. Seek a new owner decision only for a genuinely uncommitted domain policy, material rescope, frozen governance change, D145 reactivation or unapproved external/irreversible operation. Do not invent such a decision.

On tool, session or token limits, record the exact last verified Git state and next work; no claim of unattended/background continuation.

## Execution workflow

Follow the gates, leaves, dependencies and acceptance criteria in `EXEC_PLAN.md`.

Upon completion of each leaf, commit locally and export a verified Git bundle.

Rerun or expand tests only when new changes, failures, or unresolved concerns justify doing so.

## Blockers

If a requirement is unresolved, a necessary capability is unavailable, or an owner decision is required, report `BLOCKED`.

Identify the exact missing information or decision, stop only dependent work and continue any independently authorised work. Do not request routine owner reapproval.

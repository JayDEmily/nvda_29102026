## 1. System character

The admitted design is a deterministic, typed and reviewable NVDA desk-decision system. It combines market, options, temporal, posture, playbook and execution context into bounded advisory outputs. Every layer must state what it knows, where that truth came from and what authority it does not possess.

The system does not acquire live-trading authority merely because it can produce an execution-shaped answer. Unless later authority expressly says otherwise, broker connectivity, credentials, network execution, acknowledgements, booked positions, settlement, booked P&L and real-money placement remain outside the admitted design.

## 2. Context is descriptive, not self-authorising

The market-context surface may calculate or carry:

- macro score and macro-adaptive context;
- peer divergence;
- volume-spike context;
- Asia-precursor context;
- engine score;
- signal-scan summaries;
- options metadata and quote-chain context;
- gamma pressure, IV-versus-realised-volatility, skew inflection, volatility corridor and VIX-spread context;
- options-behaviour clusters, execution-context scores and compression regimes;
- OBV/VI confirmation where its tape dependencies actually exist.

Every result remains tied to explicit upstream provenance. A signal scan is an advisory typed wrapper over scan intent, configured desk-window scope and candidate counts; it is not a scheduler, daemon, broker trigger or live-trading approval. OBV/VI confirmation must remain unavailable or explicitly fenced when its tape dependency is absent. Higher-order composites cannot silently become archetypes, ladders, named playbooks, entry permissions or execution triggers.

## 3. Eligibility, readiness and conviction remain bounded

Archetype matching and tagging operate on already-existing candidates. The entry gate may veto under permission or event-window stress, but it cannot invent a playbook or grant execution authority.

Ladder construction, fill-bias adjustment, VVIX shaping and volatility-sentiment analysis produce readiness previews. VVIX may reshape the current preview ladder only; it does not create or alter broker orders. Volatility sentiment remains posture-facing.

Signal-conflict detection, confidence scoring and conviction tiers are advisory. They must not be represented as certainty, capital authority or broker approval.

## 4. Review and feedback do not self-actuate

P&L summaries, module attribution, module scoring, daily summaries, variant tracing, performance summaries, macro alignment and confidence-divergence logs are descriptive and operator-facing. They do not establish booked P&L, settlement, trade authority or an autonomous self-optimising loop.

A tail-hedge injector may emit an advisory hedge candidate. It cannot claim that protection was placed, booked or automatically routed.

## 5. Legacy and reserved subsystems

Historical imported material is evidence, not native runtime authority. It remains quarantined unless a live dependency proves that a bounded native bridge is necessary. Any such bridge must be the smallest advisory, contract or registry bridge required; it cannot recreate the historical subsystem or take ownership of serial runtime behaviour.

No native scenario-engine or observability subsystem is admitted at this authority point. Scenario identifiers and bounded scenario evidence may exist in trace, review, replay or experiment records, and ordinary components may emit operational logs or metrics. Those facts do not create a scenario engine or an observability subsystem. Any later subsystem would need separately admitted ownership and must not own business decisions, packet mutation, persistence policy or orchestration by implication.

## 6. Governing negative rule

A richer description is not a stronger authority. Context, confidence, readiness, review, trace and historical evidence may improve explanation and decision support, but none may cross into placement, capital deployment, booked state or autonomous mutation without an expressly admitted downstream authority.

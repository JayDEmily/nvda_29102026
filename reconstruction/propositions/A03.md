# A03 — Preview lifecycle and review surfaces

## P-STATE-001 — Preview state is not booked truth
- **Class / state:** execution state; active.
- **Instruction:** Execution logs, position books, trade logs and unrealised tracking are preview/advisory state only. Missing-surface markers must remain explicit, and no surface may be treated as live broker inventory, booked ledger or settlement truth.
- **Lineage:** `source_planning/2026-03-25_GATE36_EXECUTION_STATE_LEDGER_SPINE_CONTRACTS.md:L7-L24`.

## P-EXE-003 — Exit, re-entry and fill feedback remain advisory
- **Class / state:** lifecycle; active.
- **Instruction:** Partial exits, take-profit, trailing-stop, re-entry markers, fill-feedback routing and ladder continuity are preview-only. They must not imply broker-side state or live ladder management; continuity may be represented by deterministic preview identity/hash.
- **Lineage:** `source_planning/2026-03-25_GATE37_EXIT_REENTRY_CONTINUITY_CONTRACTS.md:L7-L26`.

## P-REV-001 — Review ledger and attribution are descriptive
- **Class / state:** review/attribution; active.
- **Instruction:** P&L, module trace attribution, module scoring and daily summaries are descriptive preview outputs. They must not claim booked broker P&L, settlement, or decision authority.
- **Lineage:** `source_planning/2026-03-25_GATE38_REVIEW_LEDGER_ATTRIBUTION_SPINE_CONTRACTS.md:L7-L24`.

## P-REV-002 — Feedback and hedge overlays cannot self-actuate
- **Class / state:** review/diagnostics; active.
- **Instruction:** Variant tracing/performance, feedback summaries, macro alignment and confidence-divergence logging are operator-facing diagnostics, not self-optimising loops. A tail-hedge injector may emit only an advisory hedge candidate and must not imply placement, booked protection or auto-routing.
- **Lineage:** `source_planning/2026-03-25_GATE39_REVIEW_OVERLAYS_FEEDBACK_CHAIN_CONTRACTS.md:L7-L26`.

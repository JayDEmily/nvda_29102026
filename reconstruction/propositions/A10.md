# A10 — Precursor economics, mutable policy, adjudication and geometry

## P-CTX-003 — Precursor context carries economics, not availability alone
- **Class / state:** market context; active.
- **Instruction:** Precursor packets must carry both aggregated raw-field presence and derived economic values. Financial-calendar precursor projection routes through market-state synthesis, and supportive, contradictory and hostile precursor states must be able to produce distinguishable posture and review consequences.
- **Lineage:** `source_planning/2026-03-30_GATE117_PRECURSOR_ECONOMICS.md:L1-L5`.

## P-GOV-003 — Approved mutable runtime surfaces are explicit and observable
- **Class / state:** runtime policy authority; active.
- **Instruction:** The approved mutable surfaces are `entry_gate_score_floor`, `zone_score_threshold`, `distance_to_vwap_soft_limit_pct`, `risk_vix_caution_threshold`, `risk_vix_hot_threshold`, `max_risk_per_trade`, `target_fresh_deployable_pct`, and `hedge_required`. The modifier packet resolves their operative values, execution output carries them, and review exposes the same values; no consumer may depend on hidden side channels or implied policy state.
- **Lineage:** `source_planning/2026-03-30_GATE118_MUTABLE_SURFACE_RECONCILIATION.md:L5-L17`, `:L19-L28`.

## P-PLAY-005 — Candidate adjudication is score-driven and review-visible
- **Class / state:** candidate selection; active.
- **Instruction:** When multiple playbooks are eligible, execution must rank them deterministically by scored reasons, using registry priority only as a tie-break. It emits one explicit lead, candidate adjudication records, lead score and contradiction-resolution state, all visible to review.
- **Lineage:** `source_planning/2026-03-30_GATE119_CANDIDATE_ADJUDICATION.md:L5-L13`, `:L19-L22`.

## P-EXE-004 — Governed execution geometry is template-backed
- **Class / state:** execution expression; active.
- **Instruction:** Execution templates own bounded defaults for directional bias, ladder spacing, chase distance, stop distance, take-profit distance, hedge ratio and base per-slice risk. Execution output carries explicit geometry and notes, derived contextually from the selected template and current state. Geometry may respond to event/gamma stress but must not create a second execution doctrine.
- **Lineage:** `source_planning/2026-03-30_GATE120_EXECUTION_GEOMETRY.md:L5-L13`, `:L19-L23`.

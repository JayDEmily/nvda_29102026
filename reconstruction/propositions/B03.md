## P-RES-001 — Scenario and observability disposition is conditional and unresolved
- **Successor class / state:** `unresolved`; open conditional reservation.
- **Instruction:** For each proposed native family, the admitted successor law allows only two truthful branches. **Bounded admission** is lawful only if a prior admissibility audit proves that a real bounded native owner already exists and admission would reflect current behaviour rather than fabricate capability. Otherwise the lawful result is **reserved / containment**: keep the native package absent and make that absence explicit through bounded proof/reporting rather than a placeholder package.
- **Default and exception:** B029 names reserved/containment as the expected default for both `analysis.scenarios` and `observability`, but it also preserves a bounded-admission exception. If scenarios are admitted, they remain analysis-only and may not own live trading, broad orchestration, replay/CLI/API widening or a fabricated scenario engine. If observability is admitted, it is limited to logging, metrics or correlation helpers and may not own business decisions, packet mutation, persistence rules or runtime orchestration.
- **Why selection remains unresolved:** The branch-selection evidence required by B029 is an admissibility audit over current code, proof surfaces, workbooks and reports outside the admitted 98-document corpus. B030 repeats the expected default and reports that the original outcome was substantively correct, but that conclusion depends on the same external accepted-audit history. B022 records the expected closeout state while expressly requiring a different truthful result if direct evidence disproves it. The admitted documents therefore establish the decision rule, default and branch constraints, but not which branch was lawfully selected for either family.
- **Lineage:** conditional outcomes and default at `source_successor_briefs/nvda_provisional_scenarios_observability_resolution_tranche_v1_brief_2026-04-20.md:L24-L34`; branch rules at `source_successor_briefs/nvda_provisional_scenarios_observability_resolution_tranche_v1_brief_2026-04-20.md:L144-L186`; rerun/default qualification at `source_successor_briefs/nvda_provisional_scenarios_observability_resolution_tranche_v1_full_reset_rerun_brief_2026-04-20.md:L136-L160`; expected closeout with truth override at `source_successor_briefs/nvda_final_benchmark_reconciliation_promotion_closeout_tranche_v1_brief_2026-04-20.md:L37-L49`.

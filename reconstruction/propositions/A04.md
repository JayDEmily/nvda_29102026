## P-DAT-002 — Raw capture and derived features are separate authority layers
- **Class / state:** data doctrine; active.
- **Instruction:** Direct vendor/feed observations belong only in raw capture. VWAP, realised volatility, skew, term structure, pin, gamma and participation ratios are derived features. The mixed legacy catalogue is continuity-only and non-authoritative. Playbooks must consume deterministic derived services rather than reproduce ad hoc chain mathematics.
- **Lineage:** `source_planning/2026-03-25_GATE41_RAW_DERIVED_SIGNAL_WORKBOOK.md:L5-L20`, `:L26-L28`.

## P-DAT-003 — Dense/coarse options capture and volume baselines
- **Class / state:** raw capture policy; active.
- **Instruction:** Capture same-bucket historical volume, trade-count and spread baselines. During regular trading hours capture the front two expiries densely every 30 seconds over a near-spot strike band; capture farther tenors more coarsely for curve context only. Raw option rows target expiry, DTE, strike, side, bid, ask, last, IV, delta, gamma, open interest and volume where vendor supplied.
- **Lineage:** `source_planning/2026-03-25_GATE42_VOLUME_BASELINE_AND_OPTIONS_RAW_CAPTURE.md:L5-L21`.

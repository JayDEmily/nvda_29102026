# B08 — Repo-wide source temporal standard and bounded remediation law

## P-TMP-005 — One provider-agnostic source temporal grammar governs observation, capture and persistence truth
- **Successor class / state:** `new_design_authority`; active above packet-carriage protocols; extends P-TMP-001 through P-TMP-004 without replacing their market-phase and threshold semantics.
- **Instruction:** Source-facing and persisted temporal truth uses one architecture-level grammar containing, where applicable, `source_time_raw`, `source_time_kind`, `source_timezone_raw`, `source_time_utc`, `source_date`, `captured_at_utc`, `persisted_at_utc`, `source_provider`, `source_channel` and `time_granularity`. The grammar applies to raw captures, local capture files, derived raw surfaces, persistence records, workbook-derived specimens, replay/proof surfaces and packets where relevant. It is provider-agnostic and must represent timestamp-level, date-level and mixed-date reference-ledger sources without overfitting one provider.
- **Semantic separation:** Source observation time, packet emission time, repository capture time and repository persistence time are distinct facts and must not be collapsed. Provider raw time and source granularity are preserved where relevant. No surface may invent precision, apply false UTC normalisation, or create a divergent module-local temporal grammar.
- **Lineage:** `source_successor_briefs/temporal_standard_tranche_1_normative_admission_brief_v2.md:L226-L250`, `:L266-L287`; `source_successor_briefs/temporal_standard_tranche_3_code_persistence_remediation_brief.md:L314-L357`.
- **Unresolved detail:** This corpus does not enumerate the allowed values, validation ranges or null rules for `source_time_kind`, `source_timezone_raw`, `source_time_utc`, `source_provider`, `source_channel` or `time_granularity` beyond the constraints separately admitted below.

## P-PROTO-004 — DMPV3 packet time is emission time, not source observation time
- **Successor class / state:** `bounded_amendment`; active; amends P-PROTO-003's packet-carriage semantics without changing the boundary where DMPV3 begins.
- **Instruction:** `packet.time` records packet emission time. Source observation time may differ and must be preserved separately where relevant under P-TMP-005. DMPV3 inherits rather than redefines the upstream source temporal grammar, and must not manufacture source precision that did not exist.
- **Lineage:** `source_successor_briefs/temporal_standard_tranche_1_normative_admission_brief_v2.md:L252-L264`; `source_successor_briefs/temporal_standard_tranche_3_code_persistence_remediation_brief.md:L327-L337`, `:L348-L357`.
- **Authority effect:** Explicitly disallows overloaded packet-emission fields as substitutes for source-observation truth.

## P-TMP-006 — Workbook-derived temporal truth is field-level provenance, not a synthetic same-moment observation
- **Successor class / state:** `clarification`; active; clarifies P-DAT-004, P-TRACE-001 and P-TRACE-003 for workbook-derived evidence.
- **Instruction:** Workbook dates are reference-ledger provenance. One anchor row may blend fields carrying different source dates. Workbook-derived specimens and promotions must preserve field-level provenance wherever dates differ, retain `source_date` as the lawful date-level observation where applicable, and must not masquerade as a same-moment live runtime capture. The workbook remains a governed reference ledger, not direct runtime authority.
- **Lineage:** `source_successor_briefs/temporal_standard_tranche_1_normative_admission_brief_v2.md:L277-L287`; `source_successor_briefs/temporal_standard_tranche_3_supplementary_brief_raw_truth_and_specimen_regeneration.md:L163-L170`.

## P-TMP-007 — `source_time_raw` preserves the exact source token or remains null
- **Successor class / state:** `bounded_repair`; active; repairs any normalised-value substitution permitted by a thin reading of P-TMP-005.
- **Instruction:** `source_time_raw` is the exact source-supplied time token captured from the source artefact. It must not be generated from `source_time_utc` or `source_date`. When the exact token is carried, preserve it explicitly; when it is not available on the surface, use `None`/`null` rather than fabricating a convenient normalised value. Workbook promotion follows the same rule.
- **Lineage:** `source_successor_briefs/temporal_standard_tranche_3_supplementary_brief_raw_truth_and_specimen_regeneration.md:L37-L75`, `:L147-L170`.
- **Failure rule:** Absence of an exact raw token is represented as absence, not guessed provenance.

## P-TMP-008 — Canonical temporal convergence replaces legacy aliases at admitted seams
- **Successor class / state:** `explicit_replacement`; active at the named remediated surfaces.
- **Instruction:** The active preparation/raw-bundle seam uses only the admitted canonical temporal grammar. Its internal `observed_at` field and any read of `bundle.provenance.observed_at` are retired. No alias, compatibility property, hidden fallback or dual grammar may retain that field. Workbook-derived Module 8 specimen carriage likewise retires `observed_at` and `next_event_at`; regenerated artefacts must use the canonical grammar. Other local names such as `starts_at`, `ends_at`, `recorded_at` or overloaded `emitted_at` are replaced only where they represented one of the canonical temporal facts on an admitted remediated surface; this source does not establish a repo-wide lexical ban outside those scopes.
- **Lineage:** `source_successor_briefs/temporal_standard_tranche_3_supplementary_brief_raw_truth_and_specimen_regeneration.md:L172-L198`; `source_successor_briefs/temporal_standard_tranche_3_supplementary_preparation_raw_bundle_remediation_brief.md:L75-L100`, `:L212-L265`.
- **Supersession rationale:** This is convergence, not compatibility coexistence. It replaces the legacy seam grammar but does not erase historical evidence containing old field names.

## P-PERSIST-002 — Parallel-risk observation persistence is append/write-only to live runtime
- **Successor class / state:** `clarification`; active; extends P-PRISK-001 and P-PERSIST-001.
- **Instruction:** The parallel-risk observation store records observations without becoming a live-runtime read authority. The remediated store remains write-only from the live runtime's perspective in this bounded design; persistence must not feed hidden state back into the parallel lane or serial spine.
- **Lineage:** `source_successor_briefs/temporal_standard_tranche_3_code_persistence_remediation_brief.md:L327-L357`, `:L464-L478`.

## P-ARCH-008 — The serial cognitive spine remains stateless
- **Successor class / state:** `clarification`; active; extends P-ARCH-001, P-ARCH-006 and P-PROTO-003.
- **Instruction:** Temporal remediation must not introduce hidden state into the serial spine. State, persistence and observation history remain explicit downstream or side-lane concerns rather than implicit serial-stage memory.
- **Lineage:** `source_successor_briefs/temporal_standard_tranche_3_code_persistence_remediation_brief.md:L314-L357`, `:L464-L478`.

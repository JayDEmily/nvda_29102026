# A02 — Eligibility, posture and dry-run execution

## P-PLAY-001 — Archetype and entry-gate advisory boundary
- **Class / state:** playbook eligibility; active.
- **Instruction:** Archetype matching/tagging and the entry gate operate only on existing runtime candidates. The entry gate is advisory and may veto under permission or event-window stress; it must not invent playbooks or imply execution approval.
- **Lineage:** `source_planning/2026-03-25_GATE32_ARCHETYPE_ENTRY_GATE_BRIDGE_CONTRACTS.md:L7-L22`.

## P-EXE-001 — Ladder readiness is preview-only
- **Class / state:** execution preparation; active.
- **Instruction:** Ladder construction, fill-bias adjustment, VVIX shaping and volatility-sentiment context form an advisory readiness layer. VVIX may reshape only the current preview ladder, not broker orders; volatility sentiment remains posture-facing rather than execution approval.
- **Lineage:** `source_planning/2026-03-25_GATE33_LADDER_EXECUTION_READINESS_CONTRACTS.md:L7-L23`.

## P-DEC-001 — Confidence and conviction do not grant capital authority
- **Class / state:** posture/permission; active.
- **Instruction:** Signal-conflict detection, confidence scoring and conviction-tier allocation are bounded advisory scoring surfaces. They must not claim hidden certainty, broker approval or capital deployment authority.
- **Lineage:** `source_planning/2026-03-25_GATE34_POSTURE_PERMISSION_CORE_CONTRACTS.md:L7-L21`.

## P-EXE-002 — Execution orchestration remains deterministic dry-run
- **Class / state:** execution orchestration; active.
- **Instruction:** Entry planning, position allocation, order simulation, broker adaptation, bot orchestration and execution tags may form a deterministic dry-run chain. The broker adapter must remain fenced from credentials, live acknowledgements and live bridge claims; bot orchestration must not imply a daemon, scheduler or live loop; execution tags are preview explainability only.
- **Lineage:** `source_planning/2026-03-25_GATE35_EXECUTION_ORCHESTRATION_CORE_CONTRACTS.md:L7-L25`.

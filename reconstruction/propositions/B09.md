- **Successor class / state:** `bounded_repair`; active; strengthens P-ARCH-001 and P-ARCH-007 at the preparation-to-cognition boundary.
- **Instruction:** Cognition ingress, not the serial runtime, owns translation from the prepared runtime substrate into a typed bundle of stage inputs. It uses separate deterministic temporal, regime and options/flow mappers plus a thin coordinator; calculations remain in the mapper functions. Each stage input is explicit, lawful absence is represented as absence rather than wall-clock or zero-certainty fabrication, and regime incompleteness remains explicit and typed. The serial runtime consumes the admitted bundle and must not reach into preparation internals.

## P-SERIAL-001 — The serial spine preserves six ordered, bounded and inspectable stage answers
- **Successor class / state:** `clarification`; active; extends P-ARCH-001, P-PLAY-006, P-DEC-002 and P-ARCH-008.
- **Instruction:** The serial stage order is: (1) temporal, (2) market regime, (3) options and flow, (4) posture and risk permission, (5) playbook eligibility, and (6) expression and execution.

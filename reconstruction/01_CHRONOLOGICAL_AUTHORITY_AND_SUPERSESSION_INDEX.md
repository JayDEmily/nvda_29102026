## Explicit authority and supersession chains

### Temporal phase and outward naming

- Hard elapsed-minute buckets ceased to be authoritative Step-1 truth when `P-TMP-001` introduced signal-aware temporal classification; the outward `TemporalContextOutput` remained stable (`source_planning/2026-03-25_GATE40_TEMPORAL_STATE_REPLACEMENT.md:L5-L15`, `source_planning/2026-03-25_GATE40_TEMPORAL_STATE_REPLACEMENT.md:L32-L34`).
- `P-TMP-002` made `temporal_state` canonical outward language while preserving `session_clock` only as a bounded compatibility wrapper (`source_planning/2026-03-25_GATE49_TEMPORAL_COMPATIBILITY_SURFACE_DECISION.md:L5-L18`, `source_planning/2026-03-25_GATE49_TEMPORAL_COMPATIBILITY_SURFACE_DECISION.md:L24-L36`).
- `P-TMP-005` later added source-observation/capture/persistence grammar without replacing market-phase semantics. `P-TMP-007` repaired the thin interpretation of `source_time_raw`, and `P-TMP-008` explicitly retired `observed_at`/`next_event_at` only at named remediated seams (`source_successor_briefs/temporal_standard_tranche_3_supplementary_brief_raw_truth_and_specimen_regeneration.md:L37-L75`, `source_successor_briefs/temporal_standard_tranche_3_supplementary_brief_raw_truth_and_specimen_regeneration.md:L172-L198`, `source_successor_briefs/temporal_standard_tranche_3_supplementary_preparation_raw_bundle_remediation_brief.md:L75-L100`).

### Packet protocol evolution

- Gate 54 temporarily made DMP v1 live and v2 secondary for one tranche (`source_planning/2026-03-26_GATE54_DMP_BINDING_SURFACE_DECISION.md:L5-L12`).
- Gates 57–58 explicitly replaced that bounded state: DMP v2 became the sole live producer and v1 became archived history (`source_planning/2026-03-26_GATE57_DMP_V2_CANONICAL_PROMOTION.md:L1-L5`, `source_planning/2026-03-26_GATE58_DMP_V1_RETIREMENT.md:L1-L5`).
- `P-PROTO-003` later replaced DMP v2 only at the serial runtime downstream packet-carriage boundary. Native typed carriers survive upstream and between serial stages (`source_successor_briefs/serial_runtime_parallel_risk_packet_boundary_alignment_brief_2026-04-22.md:L9-L34`, `source_successor_briefs/serial_runtime_parallel_risk_packet_boundary_alignment_brief_2026-04-22.md:L85-L113`).
- `P-PROTO-004` fixed packet time as emission time; `P-PROTO-005` limited stage packetisation to lawful stage-owned output; `P-PROTO-006` established one canonical DMPV3 envelope and builder without moving stage meaning into the shell.

### Playbook hierarchy and decision ownership

- The flat registry was replaced by the native `family -> setup_variant -> execution_expression` hierarchy through `P-PLAY-003` and `P-PLAY-004`; the flat form survives only as compatibility.
- `P-PLAY-006` later clarified that Stage 5 owns admissibility while Stage 6 owns candidate receipt, adjudication and lead selection. This is an ownership clarification, not a change to the selected outcome.

### Modifier, posture and final-risk seams

- `P-RISK-001` established one final risk join after execution synthesis.
- `P-ARCH-004`, `P-TRACE-006` and `P-DEC-002` preserved pre-mutation stage truth and separated posture hard invariants, local envelope and downstream annotations.
- `P-GOV-007` made the modifier packet authoritative and flat mutation a compatibility bridge.
- `P-RISK-002` separated raw overlay evaluation from posture-aware terminal application while preserving final outcomes and compatibility fields.

### Parallel risk and capital

- Gate 150 explicitly deferred independent parallel risk; `P-PRISK-001` later introduced it as a deterministic non-mutating sibling lane. `P-PRISK-002` amended its ingress to selected serial DMPV3 packets, and `P-PRISK-003` admitted only the bounded Stage-A bootstrap/evaluate path.
- `P-RISK-003` and `P-CAP-001` kept capital downstream of final risk and posture ownership. `P-CAP-002` then replaced a placeholder/hard-coded-base approach with the bounded supplied-capital formula, leaving units and broader portfolio semantics unresolved.

### Reserved absences and missing successor authority

- `P-RES-001` preserves a conditional branch law: reserved/containment is the stated default, while bounded admission remains possible only if external admissibility evidence proves an existing lawful owner. Because that branch-selection evidence is outside the admitted corpus, the canonical disposition remains unresolved; the constraints on either branch remain active.
- `P-ARCH-005` preserves compatibility until explicit retirement. Gates 151–156 are referenced by Gate 150 but absent from the admitted corpus; their content is not reconstructed.

No broader “later wins” rule was applied.

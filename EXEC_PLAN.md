## 1. How the gates work and are accepted

**Order:** Gate 1 (defined here, NOT_STARTED) → authorised read-only Gate 1 assessment → **formal Gate 1 exit decision** → Gate 2 assessment → **formal Gate 2 exit decision** → later gates developed from remaining work packages C–E. Drafting Gate 2 here is not opening it. The user-authorised active plan governs the read-only investigation described here; the commissioning brief remains binding as a scope/meaning source. No production implementation authority follows from this activation.

**Leaves** are semantic proof obligations, not symmetric checklist quotas. Evidence states are `VERIFIED`, `INFERRED`, `UNKNOWN`, `UNASSESSED`, `PROPOSED`, `BLOCKED` or `N/A` as applicable. Execution states (`NOT_STARTED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `READY_FOR_DECISION`, `ACCEPTED`, `RETURNED`, `BLOCKED`) are **separate** from evidence states. A leaf's `VERIFIED` findings do not automatically accept its gate. A document, Git commit, hash, test count or reviewed spreadsheet is not sufficient by itself.

**Gate-exit authority and record (mandatory for each gate):**

1. The assessing worker prepares a single gate-exit section **in this evolving plan**, with exact repository/archive/commit baseline; each leaf's claim, primary evidence and coverage; what was *not* inspected; outstanding dependencies/decisions; and proposed `ACCEPT` / `RETURN` / `BLOCK` disposition.
2. An independent reviewer evaluates the exit evidence **where the applicable authority requires one**; its identity, exact scope, findings and dispositions are recorded. Do not invent a waiver or assert that a self-review meets a mandated independence requirement. No new blanket independent-review obligation for investigation gates is inferred merely from prior implementation repair reviews.
3. **The programme owner makes or explicitly delegates the gate-exit decision** for this active investigation programme. Record the decision, date, scope, exact accepted evidence baseline and any conditions. No worker, receipt or automated validator may self-promote a gate; applicable repository implementation/promotion authority remains separately binding for later production work.
4. **`ACCEPT`** requires all mandatory propositions for that gate to be supported at their required level and no **material** unresolved authority, identity, source or semantic blocker affecting its exit claim or the next gate's entry. **`RETURN`** means remediable gaps remain; **`BLOCK`** means a required authority/input/invariant is unavailable or contradictory. A decision that merely notes a blocker is not a pass.
5. Uncertainties may be **explicitly deferred** only when their answer is *not needed* to substantiate the current gate's exit claim or safely enter the next gate; each deferred item names its owner, evidence gap, consequence and receiving later gate. An unknown directly affecting an assigned owner, effective definition, population identity, dependency-closure classification or lawful module allocation **cannot** be waived into `ACCEPT`. Deferred ≠ resolved or accepted.
6. Stop downstream dependent execution at the first missing/failed invariant; record the last established state. Gate 2 does not start after `RETURN`, `BLOCK`, or an unsigned/undecided exit. Gate acceptance is **assessment acceptance only**, never production/module admission.

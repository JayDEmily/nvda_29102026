## 1. How the gates work and are accepted

**Order:** Gate 1 (defined here, NOT_STARTED) → authorised read-only Gate 1 assessment → **formal Gate 1 exit decision** → Gate 2 assessment → **formal Gate 2 exit decision** → later gates developed from remaining work packages C–E. Drafting Gate 2 here is not opening it. The user-authorised active plan governs the read-only investigation described here; the commissioning brief remains binding as a scope/meaning source. No production implementation authority follows from this activation.

**Leaves** are semantic proof obligations, not symmetric checklist quotas. Evidence states are `VERIFIED`, `INFERRED`, `UNKNOWN`, `UNASSESSED`, `PROPOSED`, `BLOCKED` or `N/A` as applicable. Execution states (`NOT_STARTED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `READY_FOR_DECISION`, `ACCEPTED`, `RETURNED`, `BLOCKED`) are **separate** from evidence states. A leaf's `VERIFIED` findings do not automatically accept its gate. A document, Git commit, hash, test count or reviewed spreadsheet is not sufficient by itself.

**Gate-exit authority and record (mandatory for each gate):**

1. The assessing worker prepares a single gate-exit section **in this evolving plan**, with exact repository/archive/commit baseline; each leaf's claim, primary evidence and coverage; what was *not* inspected; outstanding dependencies/decisions; and proposed `ACCEPT` / `RETURN` / `BLOCK` disposition.
2. An independent reviewer evaluates the exit evidence **where the applicable authority requires one**; its identity, exact scope, findings and dispositions are recorded. Do not invent a waiver or assert that a self-review meets a mandated independence requirement. No new blanket independent-review obligation for investigation gates is inferred merely from prior implementation repair reviews.
3. **The programme owner makes or explicitly delegates the gate-exit decision** for this active investigation programme. Record the decision, date, scope, exact accepted evidence baseline and any conditions. No worker, receipt or automated validator may self-promote a gate; applicable repository implementation/promotion authority remains separately binding for later production work.
4. **`ACCEPT`** requires all mandatory propositions for that gate to be supported at their required level and no **material** unresolved authority, identity, source or semantic blocker affecting its exit claim or the next gate's entry. **`RETURN`** means remediable gaps remain; **`BLOCK`** means a required authority/input/invariant is unavailable or contradictory. A decision that merely notes a blocker is not a pass.
5. Uncertainties may be **explicitly deferred** only when their answer is *not needed* to substantiate the current gate's exit claim or safely enter the next gate; each deferred item names its owner, evidence gap, consequence and receiving later gate. An unknown directly affecting an assigned owner, effective definition, population identity, dependency-closure classification or lawful module allocation **cannot** be waived into `ACCEPT`. Deferred ≠ resolved or accepted.
6. Stop downstream dependent execution at the first missing/failed invariant; record the last established state. Gate 2 does not start after `RETURN`, `BLOCK`, or an unsigned/undecided exit. Gate acceptance is **assessment acceptance only**, never production/module admission.

### Gate 2 — Reconcile the full D001–D209 population and future responsibility proposal

**Source:** ADR-WBV2-003 **6.B**, interpreted with its semantic method, ADR-WBV2-001/002 and Gate 1's **accepted** identity/authority result.
**Entry:** A recorded `ACCEPT` for Gate 1.
**Question:** *For every D calculation, what exactly is its accepted meaning and full required input chain, and what proposed future responsibility follows without changing mathematics or silently granting production ownership?*

#### Gate 2 semantic leaves

**G2.1 — Freeze exact identity/population provenance.** At the accepted Gate 1 baseline identify original D001–D209 catalogue, original workbook, full 209-row review, V2 Pass08 114/95 proposal, the historical 95-row group allocation and 107-cross-group-reference inventory. Preserve their different evidence roles and source hashes. Verify **one canonical row for each D001–D209**, no missing/extra/duplicate identity, with original owner and historical V2 working allocation still visible. A 209-row count by itself does not prove unique coverage or meaning.

**G2.2 — Establish accepted meaning row by row.** Read the exact effective normative definition and amendments against every original and V2 row, including blank and qualification fields, notes, equations, units, source-time selection and original implementation-stage reference. Do not merge disagreements or convert a workbook label into domain authority. Record explicitly the distinctions `canonical D identity`, `current registered M01 semantic responsibility`, `historical V2 proposed owner`, `new proposed responsibility group/working label`, `future admitted owner (if any)`.

**G2.3 — Reconstruct complete semantic dependency closure.** For every D, trace direct and transitive R/D dependencies, admissible M00 source/record type, typed portfolio/candidate/context dependencies, selection and knowledge cutoffs, full history/warm-up, versioned parameters and configuration, convention and failure/refusal semantics. Treat direct lists as leads, not complete proof of strategy independence. A deterministic or multi-input calculation may nevertheless be strategy-dependent; statistical complexity alone does not disqualify a foundation candidate. If material closure is unestablished, mark `UNKNOWN/UNASSESSED` and **do not certify the classification**.

**G2.4 — Reassess strategy-independent M02 eligibility.** Apply the **accepted** ADR-WBV2-001 criteria with the newer **proposed label M02**; preserve V2's original YES/NO and reasons as separate historical assertions. Confirm descriptive meaning, lawful full predecessor closure, exact input/sampling/units and fixed non-strategy definition/configuration. A five-day historical IV measurement cannot legitimately return a five-day result from one day of history; use the actual accepted calendar, period, missing-history and refusal rules, not new invented mathematics. A proposed correction receives evidence and an explicit authority decision, never an unapproved row rewrite.

**G2.5 — Reconcile downstream proposals without prematurely canonicalising owners.** Preserve each of the historical **95 D identities**, its single **Workbook V2 proposed** group membership, and the corresponding proposed new downstream label via **Gate 1's accepted crosswalk**. These are **not** 95 independently admitted canonical software owners: the canonical *signal identity* remains singular while **actual module ownership transfers require future authority/admission**. Preserve 107 historical direct cross-group references and investigate enough to identify semantic grouping and local-chain requirements; they are **not** future runtime peer interfaces. Deeper per-module recursive self-sufficiency implementation design belongs to later work package E, but Gate 2 still must establish all **classification-relevant** dependency closure under G2.3.

**G2.6 — Record disagreements, genuine unknowns and bounded decisions.** For every challenged classification preserve `current accepted definition/owner`, `historical V2 eligibility/allocation`, `new proposed allocation`, `evidence supporting or refuting`, `decision owner`, `impact`, `epistemic status`, and `required action`. Multiple materially distinct issues for one D get separate linked issue records. No invented coefficient/provider mapping/equation/history and no silent substitution of an R identity. Material undecided classifications cannot be turned into Gate 2 `ACCEPT` by merely marking them documented; if evidence/authority remains unavailable, return `BLOCKED` or `RETURNED` with exact affected IDs and dependencies. Non-material later implementation gaps may be deferred to the named later gate.

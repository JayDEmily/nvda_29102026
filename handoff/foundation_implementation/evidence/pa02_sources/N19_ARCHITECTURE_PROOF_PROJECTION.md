# N19 — Product architecture proof projection

## Status and semantic owner

- Status: **CURRENT NORMATIVE AUTHORITY after PB-16 acceptance/commit**.
- Record ID: `N19_ARCHITECTURE_PROOF_PROJECTION`.
- Semantic owner: the bounded product-architecture proof-projection authority.
- Upstream product-proof owner: `N15_PRODUCT_PROOF`.
- Upstream product-architecture projection: `N18_PRODUCT_ARCHITECTURE_PROJECTION`.
- Generic architecture/proof grammar owner: the current architecture kernel.

N19 governs only how proof for a current product architecture implementation claim is projected across the product and architecture proof jurisdictions. It creates no product meaning, architecture identity, implementation or physical proof family.

## Normative propositions

### N19-P01 — Product architecture proof is downstream of both meaning and architecture admission

A product architecture proof claim exists only after current authority identifies the product proposition(s) being implemented and the architecture object/form that lawfully carries that implementation.

Proof, registration, file placement, package names, historical module identity, historical surface identity or green results cannot create either missing product meaning or missing architecture authority.

### N19-P02 — Architecture and product proof are distinct, non-substitutable axes

For a product architecture implementation/promotion claim, proof must satisfy every applicable obligation on both axes:

1. current-kernel architecture proof for the exact architecture propositions claimed; and
2. N15 product proof for the exact current product propositions/boundaries implemented.

Architecture proof cannot establish product behaviour. Product proof cannot legitimise wrong category, identity, ownership, dependency, public seam, state/write allocation, lifecycle, package or composition.

A green result on one axis cannot waive a required blocker on the other.

### N19-P03 — No physical product-architecture blocker exists before an implementation/allocation claim

N19 is a current normative projection, not a current physical test population.

With no current runtime `M` object, no implementation allocation and no product test-family population, there is no current runtime-owner-specific architecture blocker to execute. This false state is not a proof waiver: the obligations activate for the exact object/proposition claim when current implementation/promotion authority makes one.

### N19-P04 — Kernel architecture proof remains kernel-owned and exact

The architecture axis uses then-current kernel law and proof admission. As applicable to the claimed object/boundary it must establish the architecture propositions actually exercised, including:

- truthful registry identity/status and category/lifecycle facts;
- one bounded semantic owner and lawful component form;
- semantic-owner versus writer/reader/mechanism separation;
- declared lawful dependency direction and acyclicity where applicable;
- one typed public seam for Python-visible cross-owner use;
- truthful implementation/allocation/composition eligibility;
- package/distribution exclusion of unadmitted capability; and
- valid implementation-authority ingress where the kernel requires it.

Only the propositions actually exercised are proved. Registration or a green aggregate cannot make an untested structural claim true.

### N19-P05 — Product proof remains N15-owned and exact

The product axis activates only the N15 proof profile(s) corresponding to the exact current product propositions implemented by the architecture claim.

N19 does not broaden N15 applicability. Packet, concrete-modifier, external-effect, stronger durability/external-account and other conditional proof remain inactive until their N15/current-authority trigger is true.

### N19-P07 — N04/N05 implementation proof does not invent runtime owners

N04 and N05 remain current semantic/contract authorities, not inferred runtime modules. `N15-B01` and `N15-B02` apply when a lawful current implementation claim is made for their propositions.

N19 does not invent an architecture object to host them. Architecture proof begins only after separate current implementation authority identifies the lawful architecture form/object.

### N19-P08 — Composition proof is conditional on an actual composition claim

SCC01/N15-B06 proof becomes part of a product architecture claim only when current implementation/promotion authority claims a governed cross-owner run or composition boundary.

An application shell may coordinate admitted owners without becoming their semantic owner. Existing `A00` bootstrap status does not prove that current product composition exists or satisfies SCC01.

### N19-P09 — Carrier, modifier and technical-mechanism proof preserve trigger and ownership boundaries

A protocol/carrier architecture proof obligation is active only for a separately activated current carrier/profile and named seam. `P01` placeholder status does not trigger packet proof or implementation.

A concrete modifier architecture/proof obligation is active only after a target-specific modifier specification and implementation target are separately admitted. N21 governance alone is not a concrete implementation.

A technical `K` mechanism may be proved as a mechanism when separately admitted, but its proof cannot establish or take over N03/N12/N13 semantic state ownership.

### N19-P10 — Architecture surfaces are optional review objects, not historical continuity devices

The kernel `S` category is a view/review boundary, not automatically a package, module, implementation or completion claim. N19 does not require or create an `S` object to preserve historical family-11 surface 5.17.

A later registered architecture-assurance surface requires independent current admission and proof. It does not inherit historical eighteen-surface numbering, M14 identity, Gate-C/Gate-D status or historical proof populations.

### N19-P11 — Promotion requires all applicable axes without collapsing their ownership

A future product architecture promotion/admission claim may proceed only when every applicable current-kernel architecture blocker and every activated N15 product blocker for that exact claim are satisfied.

The current `architecture_kernel` promotion set proves only its admitted `AK-*` propositions. N19 does not insert product propositions into that immutable proof registry.

A future integrated runner/receipt may aggregate both axes only under separate current proof authority and must preserve exact family/proposition membership, failure visibility and the fact that architecture proof and product proof establish different claims.

### N19-P12 — Architecture-proof scope must be re-derived after relevant supersession

Any change to the current product proposition, N18 projection, architecture identity/category/responsibility, dependency/seam/state boundary, carrier trigger, implementation allocation or N15 proof applicability invalidates stale proof scope for the affected claim until it is explicitly re-derived and re-admitted.

Historical or previously green proof cannot be silently inherited across such a change.

### Universal dependencies

### Implementation/allocation trigger

Trigger: current higher authority makes a lawful implementation/promotion claim for a current product proposition and identifies/adopts the architecture object/form that carries it.

False state: N19 remains a normative projection; no runtime-owner-specific physical architecture/product blocker is active merely from N18/N19.

True state: applicable kernel architecture proof and exact N15 product proof activate for the claimed object/boundary.

### Conditional carrier trigger

Trigger: a named current seam is separately admitted as packetised/carrier-governed.

False state: direct typed owner seams remain baseline; packet-specific product/architecture proof is inactive.

True state: then-current protocol/carrier architecture proof plus `N15-B14` and both endpoint product boundaries become applicable.

### Conditional modifier trigger

Trigger: a target-specific current modifier specification and implementation target are separately admitted.

False state: no modifier-specific architecture/product blocker exists.

True state: architecture proof for the actual implementation form plus N15 modifier proof becomes applicable.

### Conditional external-effect/durability trigger

N12 external-effect proof and N13 stronger durability/external-account proof remain controlled by the N15 triggers in `N15-B10E` and `N15-B11D`. Architecture placement or mechanism selection alone cannot activate those product claims.

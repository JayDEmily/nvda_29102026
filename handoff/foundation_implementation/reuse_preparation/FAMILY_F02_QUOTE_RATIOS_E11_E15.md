## E1.5 — independent documentary falsification and disposition

1. **Arithmetic:** independently calculate `200/101` and `1/3` as reduced integer fractions: denominators are prime to numerators (gcd(200,101)=1; gcd(1,3)=1). No ambient Decimal division can serve as the expected oracle for a nonterminating case. Zero-midpoint and zero-sum refuse with no new result.
2. **Direct public sequencing (required future proof):** explicitly order `raw absence → M00 two-field admission → scalar first materialisation(s) → (D013 optional ratio) → later retry → source/identity perturbation → unchanged old history → writer exit → fresh readonly recovery`. For D014 use distinct R033/R036 raw path rather than predecessor snapshots. The existing M01 `public_book_quote()` provisions four fields at once, so on its own **cannot** prove the two-field admission transition or a truly absent-before-first-write state.
3. **Independent negative controls:** future-received newer quote, wrong-subject equivalent Decimal, conflicting equal-rank duplicate, intentionally incompatible predecessor version and torn/partial source page must be separately exercised with enough upstream state. Refusal on an exhausted source cursor is **not** the targeted rejection. Mutations of existing proof expected answers to obtain GREEN are forbidden.
4. **Do not conflate designs:** D011/D012 price Decimal, D013 two-predecessor exact ratio and D014 two-direct-raw exact ratio have materially different public types, identities and SQLite fault surfaces. F02 is a **reuse/audit family**, not proof that they may be combined as one implementation acceptance unit.

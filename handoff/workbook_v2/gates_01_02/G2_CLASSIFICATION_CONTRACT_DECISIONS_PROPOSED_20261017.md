## 1. Recommended scope decision B — assess required inputs separately from provider feasibility

### B1 — Exact change to the assessment proposition

For this documentary Gate 2, assess a calculation against an exact required input-contract family, independently of whether a selected commercial provider or current acquisition deployment can supply it. The classification claim is **eligibility under the specified contract**, not present source availability, confirmed provider feasibility or runtime readiness.

This explicitly replaces, for Gate 2 exit only, A1's requirement that primary provider evidence already support the proposed source's ability to express the required facts, and A2's requirement that unresolved provider callback/time/history/readiness feasibility itself remain a Gate 2 blocker. All required mathematical, identity, units, time, history, selection, state/reset and refusal meanings must still be specified. Undefined mathematics, strategy dependence or a missing classification-relevant contract cannot be deferred under B1.

Source feasibility remains UNKNOWN wherever unproved. A required contract is never relabelled evidence that IBKR, another provider, M00 or a future adapter supplies it. No undocumented provider field, snapshot marker or successful acquisition is invented. A source that cannot satisfy the contract cannot supply a valid occurrence.

### B2 — Exact affected authority and retained requirements

The bounded precedence of B1 applies to the accepted planning overlay A1/A2/A7, to D117 P6's second paragraph insofar as it requires source/calendar feasibility to establish the Gate 2 classification, and to the current Gate 2 treatment of D123's real-provider official-period mapping prerequisite. It does not alter D117 P1–P5, its R041-only dependency, D123's official-period/revision semantics, D138/D145's five-R approvals, K06/K17 arithmetic, source-admission law, or any later implementation/admission prerequisite.

D117's actual source/session/counter/calendar supply and D123's native official-period/revision mapping must be established **before a production occurrence or dependent source admission**, rather than before accepting this requirements/classification assessment. The current conflicting clauses are named here because the owner's previous acquisition-boundary direction did not itself expressly amend them. A general instruction to continue is not recorded as acceptance of B1/B2.

### B3 — Permitted parameter domains

A Gate 2 family may leave production parameter instances unbound only when the complete admissible domain, mathematical interpretation, ownership of selection, history/cutoff/refusal effect and classification invariance are explicit. The selected calculation owner, rather than a downstream strategy, owns an immutable measurement-profile version. Every production result requires a concrete complete profile selected before input evaluation; no default or adaptive choice is allowed. Consumers may request an already defined measurement version but cannot modify its meaning.

An allowed domain may contain positive finite window lengths, complete declared calendars, interval partitions, descriptive market baskets or numerical tolerances. It cannot hide an unrestricted feature/model/policy selector. Predictive/tuned weights, portfolio/candidate/order contexts, future-outcome conditioning and consumer-selected strategy regimes are excluded from foundation profiles. A change outside the admitted domain requires its own semantic classification and authority; versioning alone does not make it neutral.

This is consistent with the original brief §5 and Appendix D leaving production parameter/context instances to later work. It does not turn an unspecified domain into a completed contract. The unit contracts in §3 identify what is fixed, what may vary, and what must refuse.

## 3. Required neutral contract domains proposed for the other 26 disputed identities

**N1 — all-domain rule.** Every numerical source path is market/source-time only, subject to the exact R/D catalogue and accepted overlays; no candidate, portfolio, order intent, predictive scenario, strategy-controlled filter or policy output is admitted. All concrete contracts, parameters and source/conformity holds below are conjunctive. A missing required binding produces no occurrence. These are proposed responsibility-domain constraints, not claims that an arbitrary existing instance meets them.

### N2 — D062 and D063: scheduled events and surprise statistics

D062 consumes R284 and D001. Its universe is the complete immutable declared economic/company event-calendar scope, with no trade-activation/importance/P&L relevance filter. The profile declares the exact source/event namespaces and event classes being measured; changing the monitored scope creates a new measurement version. Within that scope select the earliest scheduled true instant strictly after as-of, using only the calendar revision visible by knowledge cutoff. Equal earliest instants have the same duration and preserve all tied event identities, rather than selecting one by query order. Return accepted seconds/minutes. Date-only events cannot supply this instant-valued calculation. D065's importance/applicability gate remains downstream and is not imported into D062.

D063 consumes R285/R286 only. One sample identifies one exact release series/measure/frequency, reference period, publication event and source/methodology namespace. Consensus is the last eligible published pre-release estimate for that event; actual is the initially released value visible at the event, in exactly compatible units. A later revision is not silently substituted. The baseline is a predeclared finite N>=2 immediately preceding comparable releases, excluding current, with the same series/frequency/units/source methodology; no selective skipping, future consensus, hindsight revisions or strategy-regime conditioning. Every required baseline release must exist. Use the accepted K04 current-minus-mean surprise divided by sample SD, denominator N-1, SD>0. The actual N and exact series/source bindings are immutable profile instances; all admissible choices remain release-specific descriptive standardisation. SRC-MACRO must establish native supply before use.

### N3 — D078–D081: one descriptive market-factor fit

Use only the accepted response D075 and covariates D071/D076/D077, one common complete aligned sample, OLS with intercept, and the existing shared-fit identity. The measurement domain permits finite rolling past windows ending at or before the stated fit cutoff. For this proposed foundation role, any state subset must be an explicitly declared retrospective predicate on those same admitted market-return observations, evaluated with information visible at each sample time. It may not refer to a strategy, a forecast/probability model, later returns/P&L, candidate selection or portfolio state; no hidden dependency is supplied through parameters.

Require a finite uniquely identifiable fit: at least four aligned observations and full column rank for the intercept plus three accepted covariates. A profile may require more complete history; it may never shorten itself when data are missing. The profile fixes sampling/return horizon, window, permitted state predicate and sufficiency before evaluation. No robust/ridge/other estimator is substituted. D079/080/081 emit the three coefficients from this same fit; D078 uses that fit's intercept/coefficients and compatible aligned current factor observations in its accepted residual equation. These are descriptive fitted sensitivities/residuals, not forecast or scenario coefficients. Concrete history/calendar/source bindings are CALC-PROFILE/SRC-CALENDAR holds.

### N4 — D082: aligned Pearson matrix

Use the accepted Pearson matrix over returns constructed from R151 market-price histories. The immutable measurement basket names exact market instruments in the NVDA/market/technology/semiconductor/declared-peer domain; no live portfolio, candidates or strategy positions determine membership. Sampling/return/calendar/window and any permitted retrospective market-only state predicate are fixed under the same restrictions as N3. Use one common complete sample and at least two observations with positive variance per member; no pairwise deletion, imputation, arbitrary weighted-correlation estimator or substituted basket. At least two distinct instruments are required. A concrete membership/window/calendar instance is required before an occurrence, but instance values do not change classification within this explicitly neutral domain. Source supply and profile proof remain holds.

### N5 — D091, D109, D110 and D121: delta family and separate joins

D091's **existing** accepted domain is finite non-overlapping absolute-R112-delta intervals with unique category identities and deterministic inclusion. A profile must specify every bound, inclusivity and label, prevent duplicate/ambiguous memberships and explicitly refuse uncovered/non-finite/out-of-domain inputs. No strategy/candidate context chooses its intervals. The five-bin numerical example in the continuation report is an optional first-instance proposal, not required acceptance or recovered authority. A category named `50` does not by its name establish ATM.

D109's P_BASIC profile must explicitly identify which interval represents approximately 50-delta/ATM, the compatible quote/contract snapshot, and exact common-strike/opposite-right/same-expiry pairing. For this proposed first domain, require exactly one complete pair within the complete declared eligible universe and refuse ambiguity. No category-only nearest-delta ranking, hidden R112 edge or arbitrary tie-break is admitted. D110 additionally requires aligned positive finite R118 model-underlying spot with exact source/underlying/currency compatibility and the accepted `100*D109/spot` equation.

D121 groups both accepted D120 components by expiry, right and D091 category, with exact per-contract joins and one common complete window. The allowed fixed horizons are 60 seconds, 300 seconds and session-to-cutoff. **Proposed first attribution mode is window-end category**, using the category at T for the whole contract-window result; rolling intervals are `(T-W,T]` in the declared receipt basis, session mode is the declared session opening through T. No trade-time category attribution is claimed. Empty/missing input is not zero. Every D120 contract must have exactly one compatible category and every selected input/version is retained.

### N6 — D102 and D111–D113: descriptive risk-neutral option-price domain

Preserve the full accepted K09 equations: D102 constrained cubic B-spline weighted least-squares total-variance fit on log-moneyness/maturity; D111 decreasing convex call-price spline and normalised non-negative second-derivative density; D112/D113 trapezoidal tail integrals. D102 remains a fitted residual/quality measurement, not an invented call-price object. D111 retains the same-snapshot D102 quality prerequisite. These are risk-neutral price-derived quantities, not D114's physical-return forecast.

The required P_SURFACE domain fixes finite ordered strike/expiry grids, exact knot/penalty and numerical tolerance interpretation, complete snapshot scope, positive maturity/spot and compatible units. An OptionRevaluationConventionVersion is required, with explicit model family/exercise style, rate/dividend/borrow/carry inputs or references, day count, numerical method/tolerance and Greek scaling. **For the proposed foundation domain every input/reference must terminate in declared lawful market/source facts or fixed non-strategy measurement conventions; no calibrated predictive return, candidate/portfolio or strategy scenario input is permitted.** The exact full input closure for the chosen convention must be assessed before accepting a concrete profile; naming the type is not that assessment.

If a proposed convention cannot satisfy that domain, its instance cannot be admitted as this foundation version and must return for a separate role/definition decision. No pricing family, early-exercise treatment, rate, dividend or solver is silently chosen in this package. The profile must fix a descriptive tail threshold/grid before input selection; strategy-selected trade barriers are not this shared version. D112/D113 require common density/grid/cutoff, correct integration limits and no unapproved extrapolation. This unit therefore remains **CONDITIONAL_PROPOSAL_WITH_CONCRETE_CONVENTION_CLOSURE_REQUIRED**, not a claim that its full chosen model is established. B1 does not waive this classification-relevant convention requirement.

### N8 — D122 and D124: expiry flow and OI concentration

Do not import D121 or an unresolved delta convention. Negative/non-integer/missing DTE refuses. Coarser trading-horizon bands are not silently selected.

D124's supported mode is the accepted **strike/expiry** grouping, not an invented spot-distance mode. Missing/duplicate/conflicting members or period context refuse. No D123 two-period subtraction is inherited. ### N9 — D138–D142 and D145: required ordered-book contract

Each record must identify one source variant, subject/contract, request/stream, generation, authoritative preserved receipt time and strictly ordered sequence scope. Preserve native categorical values and the explicit interpretation version; do not replace numeric native enums with text and represent the record as unchanged. Reconstruct each side by position and operation. Duplicate logical event identities must be idempotent or conflicting content refused. No gap, generation ambiguity, incomplete initial state, incomparable sequence, reset or reconnect may be ignored.

**Readiness means proved sufficiency of the selected visible-book scope**, not whole-market liquidity or a timeout/count heuristic. The later source/state contract must identify evidence that the initial selected rows and all mutations through cutoff are complete for the declared scope, including valid empty/short-book states where such states are source-certified. In the absence of that evidence no ready state exists. Reset invalidates reconstructed state and any dependent windows/predecessors; a new generation needs a new sufficient initial state. This is a precise required truth condition, not a claim that an IBKR callback provides an initialisation marker. B1/B4 explicitly receive feasibility of satisfying that condition in SRC-BOOK; there is no live/API task here.

D138 uses its approved five R inputs plus D011. The neutral profile fixes a positive finite level scope and matched midpoint/cutoff, constructs per-side cumulative standing size versus absolute midpoint distance, and applies accepted OLS with intercept. At least two distinct price distances with non-zero explanatory variance are required per side; incomplete depth, incompatible midpoint or invalid/negative size refuses. No ranking of candidate order sizes is introduced.

D139/D140 retain their exact catalogue R fields and SMART variant. State at the window start plus all admitted mutations is required. Use fixed 1-second/5-second/60-second receipt windows `(T-W,T]`, by side and immutable position-level buckets. Insert count/size uses the inserted event size. Delete count/removed size uses the pre-delete reconstructed row size, not a delete payload's zero. A book reset, omission or unknown predecessor invalidates the window. No undeclared price dependency is added to these position/size rate rows.

D141 retains its own complete declared R chain. The profile fixes a finite positive observation window and maximum restoration interval before observing inputs. A positive size depletion at one exact subject/side/price is matched once to its first qualifying restoration within that interval; credited size cannot exceed depletion. Preserve original book/trade evidence and do not claim an observed queue-size change proves a hidden trader's action or causal execution. No duplicate counting of one depletion through both trade and book records. The accepted ratio requires positive total eligible depletion. All thresholds are event-measurement conventions, not order/execution policy.

D142 fixes the selected depth scope, explicit pre-shock baseline, objective depletion/spread-shock predicate, recovery depth fraction in `(0,1]`, positive recovery-spread ratio and finite positive observation window. The predicate depends only on the admitted contemporaneous book/quote facts. It cannot refer to a trade candidate, risk limit or playbook. Record the first later state satisfying both accepted depth and spread recovery conditions; if no such state is seen in a complete window, no completed-resilience occurrence exists. Report accepted elapsed receipt seconds and recovered state; no filled value for censored recovery.

D145 retains approved five-R inputs and K17's exact consecutive-best-state equation. Each fixed 1-second/5-second/60-second receipt-window version requires a valid predecessor state at/before the left boundary and every ordered event through T; sum only contributions whose new-state transition is in `(T-W,T]`. No cross-generation pair, invented zero queue or first-event contribution without a predecessor is permitted. Preserve state/event lineage, reset and warm-up refusal. Standing-depth sufficiency for D138 is not substituted for consecutive-transition sufficiency here.

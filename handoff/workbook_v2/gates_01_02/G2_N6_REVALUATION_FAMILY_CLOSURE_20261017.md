## 1. Proposition and authority

Accepted B3 permits production instances to remain unbound only within a complete classification-invariant domain. Neither condition requires inventing a production parameter instance during this investigation.

The effective mathematical brief §5 K09 fixes D102's constrained total-variance surface, current-IV fitting criterion and residual; D111's direct call-mid-price convex spline, second-derivative normalised density and D102 quality prerequisite; and D112/D113's trapezoidal tail integrals. Brief §§6–9/11–17 defines explicit convention inputs, lawful M00 projections, knowledge/subject/unit matching and refusal. N6 excludes predictive return/candidate/portfolio/strategy inputs. The following closure interprets those accepted constraints conjunctively; it does not add an alternative estimator, equation, R identity or production dependency.

Three claims are separate:

1. Every permitted numerical path and configuration field has a closed admissible terminal role.
2. Every permitted valuation method preserves the same descriptive risk-neutral measurement responsibility.
3. A future concrete profile must actually satisfy those restrictions, have complete mathematics and correctness/admission evidence. **Claim 3 is not established here.** A field labelled compliant is not proof of membership.

## 2. Closed method domain — not an unrestricted model selector

For this accepted N6 responsibility, a revaluation convention is only a fully defined deterministic **current option-price reference transformation** of the admitted contemporaneous spot, strike, maturity, observed/fitted implied variance, discount/carry/dividend facts and explicit contractual exercise terms. Its use in D102 is the accepted strike-slice price-convexity condition; it is not a physical future-return forecast, expected trading payoff, selected scenario or trade value. D111 continues to use actual admitted call mid-prices; D102 residuals never become those prices.

An admissible definition must publish all mathematical operations, parameter interpretation and exercise/boundary treatment, with one immutable definition/version. It may implement its fixed valuation law analytically or through a fully specified deterministic numerical scheme. The mathematical law may contain mathematical constants, fixed unit/day-count conversions and numerical controls, but may not embed empirical forecast coefficients, discretionary feature weights or latent strategy states. A change to law or interpretation requires a new calculation-definition/convention version and semantic review under the same domain.

The only fitted quantities admitted by the current D102 rule are its accepted contemporaneous-IV total-variance surface and the stated liquidity weighting. No second, opaque calibration/training procedure may introduce additional economic state, jump/stochastic-volatility parameters, physical drift, future realised returns, return-distribution estimates or a learned market-regime predictor through the convention. Such a proposal lies outside this closed family and requires a separate definition/dependency/classification decision. Neither an apparently familiar model name nor code availability admits it.

Method choice is owned by the neutral calculation/convention authority before use. Downstream strategy, candidate or portfolio state cannot select a pricing family, exercise approximation, curve, grid or tolerance under the same measurement definition. Consumers request a previously defined measurement version; they do not supply an arbitrary model callback or override its internals. Source-rate/dividend observations are economic facts, not adjustable strategy coefficients.

This bounds **what all allowed methods mean**, not merely where their data come from. A function trained to predict realised returns remains forbidden even if every training input happened to be a market R field. An arbitrary option-model selector remains forbidden even if it is immutable. No implementation accepts a new method merely because it can return a number.

## 4. Recursive closure and exclusion argument

D102's existing signal paths are R111/R084/R093/R014/R118 plus D086. D086 terminates in its admitted expiry/time facts and D001; the accepted time conversion retains temporal-kind, calendar/expiry convention and cutoff refusal. All are market/source-time facts. The extra explicit convention-reference roles in §3 terminate only in named nominal rates/dividend market facts or fixed non-strategy mathematical/product/numerical conventions. They cannot introduce a D score, physical distribution, dealer-sign assumption, live position, order intent, candidate set or evaluation outcome.

In particular there is no path to D114 physical-return distribution, D125–D130 assumed dealer/scenario outputs, D152–D154 predictive state probabilities, D160 scenarios, D165 utility, a P_SCORE schema or an EvaluationSet. No indirect calibration may smuggle those paths back into an opaque price function. Literal profile numbers cannot encode a fitted predictor: empirical economic values require the named raw-reference roles, and other literals are limited to the stated mathematical/measurement/numerical conventions. This is why a source whitelist alone would not have sufficed, and why §2's method restriction is conjunctive.

D111 inherits the same current-snapshot D102 convention/quality lineage, but obtains its call prices from D087, with the accepted R034/R035 quote, R014/R015 strike/right and D086 maturity identity. Its accepted decreasing-convex spline and normalised second derivative introduce no forecast coefficients. The selected calls must have exercise/product semantics compatible with the claimed terminal-density interpretation. No American-to-European price transformation, unproved early-exercise equivalence or substitute synthetic call price is inserted into the accepted D087-mid-price path. If compatibility cannot be established for the selected contracts, there is no valid D111 occurrence; this does not assert that any present NVDA chain qualifies.

D112 and D113 each terminate in that same D111 density/grid/version plus their fixed descriptive terminal-price limit. Their integration and support/cutoff/refusal law cannot depend on which downstream strategy requests them. A strategy-tail calculation with a trade-dependent barrier is outside this shared definition and requires its own role/version decision.

Thus every admitted numerical path is closed to market/source-time facts or fixed neutral conventions; every economic transformation retains the accepted risk-neutral price-measurement meaning. Numerical scheme or grid differences can change a versioned value and require later correctness proof, but cannot turn an allowed member into a portfolio decision or a physical-return forecast. A proposed member that changes that interpretation is excluded by the domain, rather than counted as an eligible instance with an unexamined type label.

## 5. Assessment conclusion and concrete-profile hold

**Proposed assessment conclusion:** D102, D111, D112 and D113 qualify as conditional proposed foundation **families under this closed domain**, not as four available or accepted model instances. This is an invariant-family proof under accepted B3/N6. It does not select a production pricing family, solver, rate/dividend binding, exercise approximation or source.

Before a concrete profile is accepted, the convention authority must supply its complete exact mathematical/model definition and method, every required typed reference and conversion, exercise interpretation, numerical scheme/tolerance, source/cutoff/units, full resulting closure and positive/refusal/correctness evidence. A novel model, additional empirical parameter, unlisted source role, opaque calibration or changed density interpretation requires explicit authority and renewed classification; an implementer cannot choose it silently. B4 source-feasibility and runtime/proof holds remain independent and unresolved where unproved.

An independent review must check that the domain and exclusion argument actually establish these claims before the current four UNKNOWN classifications are changed. If the proof is returned, the rows remain UNKNOWN; the owner's package acceptance is not used as a substitute for that review.

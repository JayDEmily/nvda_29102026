#### 1. The two ownership tests ask different questions

**VERIFIED — original M01 meaning and present architecture status.** Original calculation brief §1 deliberately defines an expansive deterministic calculation owner: a `Dxxx` is within that capability when it can be computed from admitted M00 facts, D predecessors and exact versioned calculation/configuration/typed contexts. That expressly *includes* strategy-dependent calculations when their policy/model/candidate inputs are fixed and identified. The brief explicitly withholds the authority to execute trades or mutate accounts: a calculated order, action or policy-related number is still a **fact**, not an external instruction. The live `modules.yaml` registers M01 (`status: bootstrap`) as owner of D001–D209 with its own persistent occurrences, calculation/knowledge time, versions, provenance and reader/writer seams; M00 (`status: active`) retains raw observation/record truth. Neither registration nor an `implementation_allocations.yaml` entry proves the entire original calculation programme finished. No M02 or peer M03+ is registered there.

**VERIFIED — new foundation criteria, without accepting its population.** ADR-WBV2-001's classification rule and owner-directed `EXEC_PLAN.md` §0 introduce a *strictly narrower* responsibility, now provisionally named **M02** (Workbook V2 named it `M01-1`): shared descriptive/strategy-independent measures sourced through lawful M00 market/source-time records or other qualifying foundational measures. Fixed/versioned non-strategy conventions, complete history and statistical estimators may qualify; adjustable policy weights, playbook decision gates, candidate/portfolio/order-intent dependencies and chosen predictive scenario models do not qualify merely because their arithmetic is deterministic. Semantic eligibility is separate from whether implementation, reachability, durability and independent proof are demonstrated. A valid D-result must not be manufactured from insufficient history or an unqualified source; old accepted mathematics and D identity remain unchanged. The original *209-D remit cannot be renamed into M02*, because doing so would (i) assign non-foundational meaning to the new owner and (ii) silently transfer current M01 authority/lifecycle without ingress, migration and proof.

**Engineering consequence (reasoned from the accepted rules).** The future architecture needs a *classification and responsibility transition*, not a text/package rename: identify each source-backed foundation D and its contracts, retain strategy- and state-dependent D responsibilities downstream, lawfully allocate future modules, define cross-owner read/write boundaries and preserve original canonical definitions. Exactly which D rows qualify is **not decided by G1.4**; that is Gate 2. Original M01 retirement and allocation are G1.5/G1.6 and later separate authority matters.

#### 2. Original Stage 1 is a construction sequence, not a mathematical ownership boundary

**VERIFIED witness A — `D096` (ATM implied volatility by expiry):** original workbook `Derived Signals!A100:O100` and historical review record mark it **Stage 2**, kernel `K09_SURFACE_DENSITY`, while ADR1's historical eligibility review proposes it for the shared foundation. Effective catalogue row `D096` binds `R111;R112`, predecessor `D085`, `P_SURFACE`; original brief §5 K09 prescribes per-side interpolation around absolute delta `0.50` and coverage/refusal conditions. Its descriptive purpose does not change simply because its kernel was scheduled later. The historical V2 proposal is *not* final eligibility proof; missing fixed conventions or inputs remain legitimate blockers in Gate 2. Source: catalogue `D096`, V2 209-row review `D096`, G1.3 workbook comparison.

**VERIFIED witness B — `D175` (maximum permissible position risk):** original workbook `Derived Signals!A179:O179` and historical audit mark it **Stage 1**, kernel `K08_RULE_BUCKET_POLICY`; effective catalogue `D175` binds `R205;R206;R209;R208`, predecessors `D162;D174`, mandatory `CandidateStructureContext;RiskScopeContext` and `P_RULE`. Original brief §5 K08 and its later owner-approved risk-scope amendment show that a permissible position limit is a policy/context outcome, not a shared descriptive market statistic. The historical V2 review places it downstream (historical workbook `M21`), not in the foundation. This is a semantic exclusion witness, not an authorisation to move its implementation.

**VERIFIED witness C — one computational *kernel* can cross the proposed boundary:** `D164` (total expected execution cost) is `K02_ALGEBRA`, yet its effective row requires `ExecutionCostScheduleVersion`, `OrderIntentContext`, `CandidateStructureContext` and predecessor `D133`. Its mathematics can be basic arithmetic and still depend on intended order/candidate state. Compare `D001` (UTC event-time conversion), `D035` (five-day realised volatility) and `D079` (conditional historical beta): these can be foundation **candidates** if their complete meaning, source selection and non-strategy conventions are established, despite time-window/regression complexity. Sources: catalogue rows and original brief §§5–7; ADR1. Neither `Kxx` family nor Stage-1/Stage-2 implementation schedule is a reliable allocator of future semantic ownership.

**Design conclusion:** Preserve old stage/progress evidence for reuse assessment, but do not equate “Stage 1 implemented” with “M02 implemented”, or “Stage 2 pending” with “not needed by M02”. Stage 1 contains strategy-policy calculations; prospective M02 contains some calculations historically scheduled for Stage 2. Whether a *specific code path* is correct/reusable remains C–D work, not established by these example rows.

#### 3. R and D are identity types, not a market-data-versus-strategy taxonomy

**VERIFIED — `Rxxx` does not mean exclusively a market quote.** ADR1's M00 section explicitly includes account/risk, position, order/execution and operational-clock records in M00's raw truth, alongside market observations. The original brief §§6 and 9 defines `RawSignalProjectionMapping` as an exact field-level, versioned conversion from a canonical R identity to a provider/native-record field: a record's `raw_signal_catalogue_ids` metadata by itself does not identify which numeric field is meant. The brief §5's D164 rule explicitly distinguishes **R233 realised post-trade commission** from pre-trade expected costs, proving a non-market raw record belongs to the allowed R namespace. M00 owns that raw fact; any separate decision about how a derived result may use it is not settled by the letter `R`. M00's public facet must carry the exact occurrence ID, native value/units, field mapping, temporal kind, receipt ordering and history; it must not perform D mathematics or invent unavailable event time.

**VERIFIED — `Dxxx` means canonical calculated meaning, not necessarily “derived market data”.** Examples directly in the effective catalogue/brief: `D035` is measured five-day realised volatility; `D114` is a fitted conditional *physical-return distribution* with model parameters; `D133` is expected fill slippage for a particular intended order (`OrderIntentContext`); `D165` is per-candidate risk-adjusted utility; `D175` is maximum permissible candidate risk; `D188` is current-position continuation value; `D192` is a policy-ordered action **calculation fact** (not an order action); `D193` is realised transaction cost from execution/position facts; `D206` is out-of-sample decision performance using an `EvaluationSet`; `D209` is a scheduled-update trigger fact with `RecalculationSchedule`. All share the canonical D namespace under original M01. Thus deterministic reproducibility and a `Dxxx` label **do not establish** descriptive market-measurement eligibility. Sources: the named catalogue rows, original brief §5 K08/K10/K12/K13/K15, and §6 context definitions.

**Classification boundary:** A D using strategy-dependent context cannot claim shared M02 meaning by relabelling its input as R or by freezing a trading-policy coefficient. Conversely a fixed, versioned *non-strategy* comparator, clock or history convention need not make a genuine descriptive estimator strategy-dependent. Complete input/provenance/temporal/parameter closure is a Gate 2 obligation; G1.4 gives falsifiable witnesses, not a blanket reclassification of the 209 identities.

#### 4. Downstream independence requires five real interfaces/boundaries, not directory moves

- **Contract and semantic boundary (VERIFIED requirement):** `docs/architecture/OWNERSHIP_AND_COMPONENT_LAW.md` AK-OWN-001/AK-FORM-001 and ADR2/Pass08 require each future downstream peer to own its D outputs and assumptions, declare allowed foundational M00/M02 input types, exact canonical output identity, selection/as-of/knowledge rules, versioned configuration and failure semantics. The owner-approved V2 rule is stricter than generic `DEPENDENCY_LAWS.md` AK-DEP-001 (which otherwise permits declared acyclic M-to-M edges): **between future downstream peers, none**. A requirement on one peer's action/scoring context cannot be smuggled into M02 by calling it reusable utility.
- **State and persistence boundary (VERIFIED requirement):** AK-STATE-001 separates the meaning/writer of durable occurrences from the PostgreSQL adapter and other readers. The existing M01 `modules.yaml` entry and original brief §§10–12 specify M01-owned durable D-result/version/lineage records and a write boundary independent of M00. Future M02 and downstream peers must establish *their own* lawful writer, read, state and compatibility dispositions; physically copying `M01` PostgreSQL rows or retaining M01's writer for all successors does not establish semantic transfer. Different owners may use the same underlying technical PostgreSQL service without sharing write ownership.
- **Public seam and dependency boundary (VERIFIED requirement):** `PUBLIC_SEAM_LAW.md` AK-SEAM-001 and `DEPENDENCY_LAWS.md` require explicit typed consumer interfaces and declared lawful imports. A Python file moved under another package can still import an old peer's private helper, read private data, or assume old M01-wide dependencies. The intended downstream peers must not do so. Public APIs are designed/authorised contracts, not permissions supplied by import syntax; the existing `src/nvda_desk/derived_signal_calculation_truth/api.py` defines `CalculationRequestV1`, `DerivedSignalOccurrenceV1`, `RawInputLineageV1`, `RawProjectionReaderV1`, `get/list` read logic and typed contexts, showing existing engineering seams to assess for reuse, **not** evidence that a new M02 peer contract is already admitted.
- **Version, occurrence identity and historical lineage boundary (VERIFIED requirement):** The original brief §§6, 11–12 and present `api.py` `DerivedSignalOccurrenceV1` require exact D/result identity, versioned contexts, M00 observation/projection mapping/unit conventions, predecessor occurrence IDs, as-of/window and knowledge cutoff, append-only successor treatment. A future foundation result must not silently preserve an R identity while changing meaning, reinterpret a past D result under a new policy, overwrite earlier accepted occurrence lineage or claim five-day history from one day. Extraction/reuse needs a compatibility decision for stored results and consumers; such decisions are future authority, **not** automatically achieved by code separation.
- **Local-intermediate boundary (VERIFIED design; implementation UNASSESSED):** ADR2 and Pass08 require a downstream peer to implement and test locally any *strategy-dependent* intermediate calculation it needs from another peer's historical D-lineage, reaching lawful M00/M02 ingress. Its intermediate has peer-private identity/configuration/version/lineage: it is **not** a second canonical `Dxxx` occurrence or a second public canonical owner. Shared descriptive M02 calculations are reused through M02's authorised public seam, **not** duplicated as private market-canonical output. Per-peer full closure, code adaptation and independent execution proof belong to later work E.

**Concrete cross-group witness (VERIFIED historical design conflict).** `handoff/workbook_v2/pass06_modules/PASS06_107_CROSS_MODULE_D_REFERENCES.csv` explicitly records `D165` in historic `M19` depending on `D164` in historic `M18`, and `D192` in historic `M27` depending on `D188/D190/D191` in historic `M26` and `D175` in historic `M21`. The *current* original M01 graph can name these canonical D predecessors because one M01 owns the graph. Treating those historical references as runtime calls between independent V2 peers would violate ADR2's no-peer-dependency design. The Pass06 `Resolution` string still speaks of “dependency migration”; **Pass08 explicitly supersedes that wording**, reinterpreting the 107 references as historical D-lineage only. A lawful future design must examine each required chain, determine what is foundational and what must be recomputed locally, and keep canonical D ownership singular. The nominal future relabelling (`M03+`) has not been admitted by allocation authority, so the historic `M18/M19/M21/M26/M27` here are deliberately historical labels, not new runtime IDs or an execution order.

#### 5. What is actually established about reusable engineering (and what is not)

**VERIFIED by static source inspection only:** `src/nvda_desk/derived_signal_calculation_truth/api.py` contains typed immutable contexts for candidates, sets, position, order intent, evaluation and scheduling; typed `CalculationRequestV1`, `DerivedSignalOccurrenceV1` and `RawInputLineageV1`, a raw-projection protocol, read facade and persistence protocol. `src/nvda_desk/derived_signal_calculation_truth/_stage1.py` explicitly groups K01–K08/K16/K17 under one historical Stage-1 implementation (`STAGE1_KERNEL_IDS`) and has row-specific branches including D175; `src/nvda_desk/derived_signal_calculation_truth/_postgres.py` declares M01-specific persistence boundaries. These source facts explain why some useful mechanisms may be reusable **with separation** rather than replaced outright, and why the same owner currently mixes generic measurement machinery with policy/context machinery. They are neither live-path acceptance nor evidence that an independent future M02 or peers exist. No implementation progress percentages or claims about correctness of individual kernels follow from this limited inspection.

#### 6. G1.4 assessment disposition

**G1.4 claim satisfied at investigation level — `READY_FOR_DECISION`, not gate acceptance.** The record establishes from current module registry, original M01 brief/effective rows, owner-approved V2 boundary rules, architecture laws and direct code/lineage examples (a) why original M01's 209-D deterministic remit is broader than proposed shared strategy-independent M02; (b) why Stage 1 and M02 populations are not interchangeable; (c) why `R`/`D` identity labels do not classify meaning or eligibility; and (d) why downstream autonomy requires independently defined contract, state, typed seam, version/lineage and local intermediate arithmetic rather than folder moves. No current mathematical owner, definition, workbook label, package, ID allocation, test or runtime behaviour has been changed. No need to open G1.5/G1.6 or Gate 2 to complete this **conceptual mismatch proposition**. The gate remains `IN_PROGRESS`, and its formal exit decision still belongs to the programme owner after all leaves.

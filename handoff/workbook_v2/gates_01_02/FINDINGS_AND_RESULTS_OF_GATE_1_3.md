#### 1. Why V2 exists: a real classification/ownership problem, not a workbook facelift

The master commissioning brief `handoff/workbook_v2/decisions/ADR_WBV2_003_V2_INVESTIGATION_COMMISSIONING_BRIEF_VERBATIM.md` §§1–3 and 6.A–B says the old **"derived signals"** designation was overly broad. The original accepted M01 remit is the *single* D001–D209 deterministic calculation programme, including descriptive market measurements **and** scored models, candidate/portfolio economics, risk and strategy decisions and evaluation. Deterministic execution therefore cannot by itself identify a shared strategy-independent measurement. The original M01 Stage 1/Stage 2 distinction is a **kernel implementation-scheduling partition**, not a source/strategy semantic boundary. The owner reset V2 to examine the boundary before choosing what to implement, reuse or retire. Later on 15 October the owner directed the target architecture to keep M00, treat M01 as a transitional extraction source for later lawful retirement, call the **proposed shared foundation M02**, and use **proposed M03+** for independent downstream peers. This later direction changes the *working future naming*, not the older V2 source records, effective mathematical definitions or the current production registry.

**Original workbook primary evidence:** `handoff/m01_completion/authority/NVDA_80_STEP_ACADEMIC_EVIDENCE_AUDIT_WITH_SIGNALS_ADDENDUM_81_94.xlsx` (SHA-256 `519ea9449297d564ae4a02afc1bf4e60e4471631c81a0842620f9fb60bb4f9ae`); `Derived Signals!A4:O213` describes D001–D209 with intended input dependencies, concepts, units, windows, purposes, qualifications and original research steps; its first two rows explicitly state **no equations are written in this tab**. `Raw Signals!A4:Y327` lists 323 source R records with provider, fields, time and status (including non-market records). `Method & Legend!A1:B16` warns that academic support is not individual equation, implemented code or realised trading performance proof. #### 3. Verified proposal accounting versus unverified meaning

Read-only comparison of original/review Excel cells and the research CSVs shows: `M01_1_FULL_209_ROW_ELIGIBILITY_REVIEW.csv` has **209 rows**, 114 `YES` and 95 `NO`, every row carrying review status `WORKBOOK_V2_REVIEW_NOT_M01_AUTHORITY`; the V2 `M01-1 Derived Raw!A6:A119` identities equal those **114** review YES identities as a set. The 95 historical `PASS06_95_D_MODULE_ALLOCATIONS.csv` `(D, working module, group)` triples agree exactly with V2 `M02-M31 Signal Allocation!A5:E99`; the 30 register identity/group/count/membership tuples agree exactly with V2 `M02-M31 Module Register!A5:E34`. G2.1–G2.6 must do that assessment from authoritative definitions and dependencies.

#### 4. Exact four-way status distinction and decision consequence

1. **Accepted calculation meaning:** original M01 brief, **effective** normative D001–D209 catalogue and individually approved amendments from G1.2. The old workbook preserves original concept/provenance, not a competing replacement for amended mathematical authority.
2. **Owner-approved design criteria:** ADR1 strategy-independence qualification; ADR2 no peer runtime/private-data dependency, local intermediate duplication and unique canonical D outputs; Pass08 clarification; newer owner's *target naming and intended future responsibility*. Approval of a *criterion* does not validate which exact rows satisfy it.
3. **Proposed workbook assignments:** 114/95 historical eligibility, 30 `M02–M31` historic labels, 40-family and 13-class review taxonomies. These retain their historical identities. Under the newer proposed target the shared foundation is called `M02` and downstream peers are provisionally `M03+`; no mechanical `M02→M03` shift is admitted.
4. **Registered production semantic responsibility:** `config/architecture/modules.yaml` and `config/architecture/implementation_allocations.yaml` reviewed under G1.1 still show M00 registered `active` and M01 `bootstrap` as the original 209-D owner/responsibility, **no registered M02+**. No workbook worksheet, audit, commit or design ADR itself changes that registered allocation.

**Engineering decision:** retain the historical 114/95 distribution as a specific **Gate 2 hypothesis to challenge**, not as an accepted identity/meaning/population-by-definition result. Gate 2 must use full effective definitions, all notes/blanks/qualifiers, typed contexts and transitive history before confirming, changing or blocking each proposed foundation/downstream classification. Preserve one canonical D identity even when future peer-private maths duplicates an intermediate chain. G1.5 separately resolves lawful module ID/lifecycle and eventual M01 retirement; G1.6 retains unfinished old obligations. No upstream source files or workbooks have been changed by this assessment.

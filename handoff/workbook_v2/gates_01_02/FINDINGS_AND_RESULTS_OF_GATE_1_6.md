## 1. Substantive result: preserve the old *propositions*, not the old execution order

The old M01 project was designed to finish **all 209 canonical D calculations inside registered M01**, proceeding through Stage 1 (174 kernel rows; 144 closed and 30 with cross-stage predecessors), Stage 2 (K09–K15) and Stage 3 (complete persistent graph and independent acceptance). That schedule did not complete. Its mathematical and proof obligations **do not disappear** because the owner now intends to retain M00, extract a shared descriptive M02, allocate proposed independent M03+ peers, and then retire M01.

Conversely, finishing old monolithic Stage 1/2/3 *as that particular runtime architecture* before implementing successors is **not** justified by the newer owner-approved design direction. The correct transition carries the obligations and accepted bounded engineering evidence forward **per D and per required mechanism**, with their future place contingent on Gate 2 semantic classification and later independently approved implementation ingress. An old Stage-1/Stage-2 label is not a future module boundary.

Old F2 was independently accepted **only** for its controlled observation-correlation scope; F3 was a Worker checkpoint; F4/full Stage 1, Stage 2 and whole M01 remain unaccepted. R01/R02 repairs for D025/D099/D100/D120 were separately bounded, with R02 independent semantic review, **not** entire M01 and **not** supported-runtime proof. G01's historical 114/95 classification and convention schedule never received foundation admission. These are different propositions; none cancels the others.

# Dependency testing windows — proposed admission contract

No case is released. The delta gives all 35 exact proof IDs, contract leaves, transitive leaf prerequisites, parent proof IDs and original finite candidate case IDs/paths/variants. G1 11, G2 12, G3 4 and G4 7 describe obligation counts, not physical case counts. D145 is the remaining paused obligation. The 74 prospective cases retain their original limits (60 signal, up to 14 supporting); proposed admission is not execution authority.

Before each individual proof in any window the separate Tester must record ALL of:

1. Exact clean candidate commit and tree; accepted owning source/meaning/profile version and SHA-256; public ingress and source occurrence/member identities, units, clocks, selection, cutoffs and completeness; exact claim/generation/pointer and runner hashes. Bind every UNRESOLVED field; historical source commits are provenance only.
2. Independent documentary clearance of every listed source-contract leaf, plus all direct raw/derived edges from the canonical coverage register/catalogue. The transitive leaf list is a minimum execution ordering constraint, not a substitute for the catalogue. For each required parent proof bind accepted candidate/profile/environment/generation, raw outcome hash and independent verdict, or exact reusable unchanged historical proof with invalidation analysis. Untested parents do not qualify. Additional D072–D074/history, D035/D036/D087 or M00 changes require their own finite prior admission; the seven support pairs give no blanket waiver.
3. Actual Python executable/version/hash, dependency identity/lock and wheel hashes, SQLite loaded binary/hash/source ID/options and required features, accepted compatibility disposition, exact runner and backend. Current architecture CI or an isolated mechanism probe cannot fill product qualification. Unsuitable/missing evidence blocks only dependent execution.
4. Owner-adopted coordinated schedule/transport/role package; designated separate Tester; independently restorable candidate and all required refs/non-Git assets. Exact finite admitted node IDs including parameter variants, case count/ceiling, source-derived independent oracles, success/reached-refusal and public durable readback. Exact collected-node equality and retained raw outcomes are required before acceptance; the existing runner alone is not an R2 case-budget enforcer. No hidden loop/parameter case expansion.
5. Tester verdict after execution, with original failure and bounded material repair lineage retained. Failed reruns require relevant repair; successful reruns require precise material invalidation. L32 must reuse unchanged accepted results.

# 3. Implement immutable capture-scope policy versions
Treat `raw_signal.capture_scope_policy` as configured context declaring which 30-second schedule universe exists, not as a provider fact or observation. `CaptureScopePolicy.v1` requires `scope_policy_id` as opaque configured-domain identity, positive `scope_policy_version`, slot-aligned UTC `activation_ts_utc` at nominal `:00` or `:30`, optional exclusive slot-aligned `deactivation_ts_utc` later than activation, and immutable schema-identified owner-local `eligibility_policy_ref` retaining exact provider/source/subject selectors and mapping as policy lineage. Absence of `deactivation_ts_utc` means only that no deactivation is declared, not perpetual service. Permit at most one active policy version at an instant in this slice. A new version takes effect only at its declared boundary and must not rewrite an earlier schedule universe. Concrete scope contents/provider eligibility mappings remain Unknown.

# 4. Generate scheduled capture occurrences only from owed slots
Treat `raw_signal.scheduled_capture_occurrence` as one owed nominal half-open UTC slot for one active policy version; it is not an observation, snapshot, provider event or scheduler-wake occurrence. `ScheduledCaptureOccurrence.v1` requires opaque M16 `capture_occurrence_id`, identity-and-version `scope_policy_ref`, nominal UTC `slot_start_utc`, and `slot_end_utc` exactly 30 seconds after start; declare concept/schema identities separately rather than reusing them as occurrence identity. Treat `(scope_policy_ref, slot_start_utc)` as the stable slot/idempotent-retry reference admitting at most one capture occurrence, not as the opaque occurrence ID. Slots are exactly `[start,end)`. Owe no slot before activation or at/after deactivation. Never reinterpret scheduled time as scheduler-wake, collector-receipt or provider-event time.

# 6. Preserve provider record/delivery boundaries and independent observation validity
A raw observation is one actually received provider logical record; where the provider exposes no separable record, the delivery itself is one raw-observation occurrence. For one delivery containing separable provider records, create one raw-observation occurrence per provider record and share the delivery reference. Treat a native field-update callback as one observation when it is the provider record. Never coalesce separate records/deliveries and never split one provider record into repository-created field occurrences. Preserve a raw observation with unchanged meaning and identity even when no eligible active capture scope exists. Capture occurrence and raw observation admission are independent and neither may fabricate the other.

Keep assessments append-only in an acyclic single-predecessor chain; never overwrite history and define no final assessment. `unknown_not_established` means presence not established at the boundary; `zero_received` means no eligible live observation receipt established there; `one_or_more_received` means at least one eligible receipt with no exact count. None asserts health, market silence, provider-event absence, validation/quarantine result, observation validity, completeness, capture closure or finality.
